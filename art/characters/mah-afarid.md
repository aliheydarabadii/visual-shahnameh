# Mah-Afarid · ماه‌آفرید

Iraj's handmaid, named in 07.12. She is pregnant with his daughter. She is not Manuchehr's mother: the daughter is.

First painted in: 07.12 (`art/prompts/07-12.md`).

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

A young handmaid at miniature scale, pari-faced only as a compliment: no wings, no halo. A rounder face than Iraj's daughter, dark almond eyes, a small closed mouth, black hair under a saffron veil worn back so the face is clear. A loose malachite-green robe with tiny gold flowers, modest, fully clothed, never nude. One small pearl at the ear. No crown, no farr. She always faces the viewer's left.
