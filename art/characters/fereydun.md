# Fereydun · فریدون

Abtin's son and Faranak's. An infant in 06.03, sixteen in 06.04, a young uncrowned then newly crowned leader in 06.05. Later chapters keep this face.

First painted in: 06.03 (`art/prompts/06-03.md`).

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

A young king at miniature scale, of Abtin's house. Oval face, high forehead, dark almond eyes, a long straight nose, a small firm mouth, a faint vertical line between the brows. A short neat black beard along the jaw, not pointed and not white. Dark hair in two long locks past the shoulders. A low gold crown-cap, never a tall crown and never a turban: a smooth gold skullcap with one small plain gold disc at the front and one short black heron plume. An ivory kaftan patterned with a thin malachite vine, a narrow vermilion sash, ivory trousers, bare ankles. No leopard skin. A soft pale-gold disc of farr behind the head, always dimmer than the painting's central light. He always faces the viewer's left.
