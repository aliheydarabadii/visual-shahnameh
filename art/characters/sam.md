# Sam · سام

Sam, son of Nariman. A champion of Manuchehr's right wing. Not Zal, and not Rostam. He is a grown man here, dark-haired.

First painted in: 07.14 (`art/prompts/07-14.md`).

## Prompt line

Sam, son of Nariman, a tall champion at miniature scale, narrower in the shoulder than Qaren. Long face, dark almond eyes, a black beard forked in two points. Dark hair. A pale silver helmet with a mail aventail and no plume. Silver-grey ring-mail over an ivory tunic, a deep violet short cloak, a gold belt. A straight sword with a plain steel hilt and a plain steel mace with a round head. No crown, no farr. He always faces the viewer's left.
