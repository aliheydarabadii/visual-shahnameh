# Qobad · قباد

Young scout and outrider of Manuchehr's host. He carries Tur's insult back to the king. Leaner and younger than Qaren. No crown.

First painted in: 07.16 (`art/prompts/07-16.md`).

A young scout at miniature scale, lean, clean-shaven, a narrow jaw, dark almond eyes, a long straight nose. No crown, no fillet, no farr. A small steel cap wrapped in a turquoise cloth.
