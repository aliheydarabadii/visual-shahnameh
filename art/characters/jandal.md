# Jandal · جندل

Fereydun's envoy. A lean man past middle age, clever and plain-dressed beside the princes. He circles the world, finds Sarv of Yemen, and carries the answer home. No crown and no weapon out.

Jandal, Fereydun's envoy, a lean man past middle age at miniature scale. Warm olive pigment, flat, not photoreal. A grey-streaked dark beard cut short, deep-set almond eyes, a lined forehead, a long nose, a calm mouth. An indigo travel coat with a narrow gold edge, ivory trousers, a plain brown wrapped cap with no jewel and no crown. A leather satchel. No weapon drawn. He always faces the viewer's left.
