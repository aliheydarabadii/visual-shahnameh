# Iblis · ابلیس

The tempter in Zahhak's story. He is always in a human disguise. He is not Ahriman: do not use the faceless smudge from `ahriman.md` for him.

First painted in: 05.02 (`art/prompts/05-02.md`).

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

Iblis in a human disguise, miniature scale, never a monster, never a naked demon, never smoke with a face. A smooth oval face, no beard, amber-brown eyes set a little too wide, a thin smile that does not reach the eyes. Hair always covered. The tell that never changes: a thin vermilion cord knotted twice around the left wrist. No horns, no wings, no halo. He always faces the viewer's left.
