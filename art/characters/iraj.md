# Iraj · ایرج

Youngest son of Fereydun, by Arnavaz. The fairest of the three, and the only one without a beard at the Yemen embassy. Unnamed in 07.02–07.03. Ivory and turquoise are his colours.

First painted in: 07.02 (`art/prompts/07-02.md`).

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

The youngest son of Fereydun, a youth at miniature scale, slightly shorter than his brothers, the fairest of the three. The same long narrow face, high forehead, dark almond eyes, long straight nose and small closed mouth, with a smoother unbearded jaw. Dark hair in two long locks past the shoulders under a thin gold fillet with one small turquoise bead. An ivory robe with a pale-gold hem and a turquoise sash. No crown and no sword. Empty hands. A soft pale-gold disc of farr behind the head, always dimmer than the painting's central light. He always faces the viewer's left.
