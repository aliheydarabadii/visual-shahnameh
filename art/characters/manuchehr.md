# Manuchehr · منوچهر

Iraj's grandson, born in 07.12 and crowned by Fereydun in the same section. Later chapters show him as king: keep this face.

First painted in: 07.12 (`art/prompts/07-12.md`).

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

A youth of Iraj's house at miniature scale. Oval face, dark almond eyes, a long straight nose, a small closed mouth, cheeks a pale arghavan rose, no beard. Dark hair in two locks to the shoulders, held by a thin gold fillet with one small plain gold disc. An ivory tunic under a pale wine-coloured short coat with a turquoise hem. When enthroned, a narrow turquoise diadem: a simple band with one oval turquoise and one plain gold disc, never a tall crown. A heavy gold mace with a small flat ox-head, never larger than his torso, set beside him only when the scene arms him. A soft pale-gold disc of farr behind the head, always dimmer than the painting's central light. He always faces the viewer's left.
