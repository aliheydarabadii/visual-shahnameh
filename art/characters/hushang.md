## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

A boy of the same house, younger and smaller than Siyamak. Rounder cheeks, the same almond eyes, the same long nose, no beard. Dark hair cut to the jaw, held by the same thin gold fillet. Leopard skin worn as a short belted tunic, the same pale tawny and the same black ringed rosettes, with ivory trousers. A short knife with an ivory hilt at his belt, sheathed except when he strikes the div. He always faces the viewer's left.
