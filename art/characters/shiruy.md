# Shiruy · شیروی

The lion-slayer in Manuchehr's host. Not a Sasanian prince.

First painted in: 07.14 (`art/prompts/07-14.md`).

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

Shiruy the lion-slayer, a compact champion at miniature scale. A square jaw, a short curled black beard, dark eyes. An open iron helmet. Fine ring-mail, a small vermilion shoulder cloth, ivory trousers. A round shield with a flat gold lion silhouette and no letters. A straight sword, no mace. No crown and no farr. He always faces the viewer's left.
