# Kaveh · کاوه

The blacksmith. Plaintiff in Zahhak's court in 06.05. His plain leather apron becomes the Kaviani banner.

First painted in: 06.05 (`art/prompts/06-05.md`).

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

A blacksmith at miniature scale, middle-aged, broad in the shoulder but still a small manuscript figure, never a giant and never bare-chested. A square jaw, thick dark eyebrows, deep-set dark eyes, a short dense black beard cut square across the jaw, a little grey at the temples. Short dark hair. No crown, no diadem, no fillet, no farr. A plain dark-brown leather apron, unornamented, over an indigo short tunic and ivory trousers. Bare forearms. A small smith's hammer with a plain steel head at his belt. He always faces the viewer's left.
