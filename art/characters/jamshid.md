Jamshid, Tahmuras's son, at miniature scale. The house face, younger than Tahmuras: dark almond eyes, a long straight nose, a small closed mouth, a high smooth forehead. A short neat dark beard along the jaw only, not a long beard. Dark hair in two locks to the shoulders, held by a thin gold fillet. A narrow gold diadem, a simple band with one small plain gold disc at the front, never a tall crown and never a turban. A saffron brocade robe with small gold rosettes, a turquoise hem, a malachite sash, ivory trousers. No leopard skin. A soft pale-gold disc of farr behind the head, always dimmer than the painting's central light. He always faces the viewer's left.
