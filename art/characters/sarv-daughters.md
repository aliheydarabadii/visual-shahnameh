# The daughters of Sarv · دختران سرو

Three sisters of one mother and one father. The poem says their height and faces match so nearly that one cannot be told from another, and that in this part of the story they are still unnamed and veiled. They are told apart only by the sash.

First painted in: 07.02 (`art/prompts/07-02.md`).

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where any of them appears. If only one sister is in the painting, add which sash, and do not change this line.

The three daughters of Sarv, young women at miniature scale who share one face. Oval face, dark almond eyes, a small closed mouth, the same warm brown pigment as their father. Dark hair in two braids. An ivory robe. A sheer gold gauze covers the lower face while they are still ungiven. They are told apart only by the sash: eldest turquoise, middle vermilion, youngest saffron. No crown. Each always faces the viewer's right.
