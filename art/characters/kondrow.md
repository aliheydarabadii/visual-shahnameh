# Kondrow · کندرو

Zahhak's steward. He keeps the palace while Zahhak is away, flatters Fereydun, then carries the news back. He is not a warrior and not a king.

First painted in: 06.06 (`art/prompts/06-06.md`).

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

Kondrow, Zahhak's steward, at miniature scale. A short middle-aged man, round face, thin brown beard to the jaw, narrow eyes, a small mouth. No crown, no fillet, no plume, no farr. A rust-brown knee-length coat, an ivory skullcap with no disc, ivory trousers, and a plain cloth of office at the belt with one geometric star and no writing. A slight stoop. No weapon. He always faces the viewer's left.
