# The mobed envoy · موبد فرستاده

The sharp-minded mobed Salm and Tur send to Fereydun in 07.06. He is not a king and not the dehqan. No name is given in the poem.

First needed in 07.06.

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

A middle-aged mobed, not a king. A brown-grey beard, a calm oval face, dark eyes, no diadem and no farr. An indigo coat, a plain brown felt cap, a dusty undyed ivory riding-cloak, a thin ivory staff. A small satchel patterned only with a geometric star, never with letters. He always faces the viewer's left.
