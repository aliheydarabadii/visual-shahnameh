# Kayumars · کیومرس

The first king. Dies at the end of 02.01.

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

An old king at miniature scale. Long narrow face, high forehead, dark almond eyes, a long straight nose, a small closed mouth. An ivory-white beard in three points: two side locks and one long centre lock to the chest. A narrow gold diadem, a simple band with one small plain gold disc at the front. Never a tall crown, never a turban. A leopard-skin mantle, pale tawny with black ringed rosettes, over an ivory tunic with a thin turquoise hem. Bare ankles. A soft pale-gold disc of farr behind the head, always dimmer than the painting's central light. He always faces the viewer's left.
