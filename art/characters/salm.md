Eldest son of Fereydun, by Shahrnaz. Unnamed when he first appears in 07.02; named when the world is divided. Tallest of the three brothers. The malachite robe is his colour through the later wars: keep it when he is king of Rum.

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

The eldest son of Fereydun, a youth at miniature scale, the tallest of the three brothers. Long narrow face, high forehead, dark almond eyes, a long straight nose, a small closed mouth. A short neat dark beard. Dark hair to the shoulders under a thin gold fillet with one small malachite disc. A malachite-green robe over ivory, a gold belt, ivory trousers. No crown. A sheathed straight sword with a plain gold hilt. A soft pale-gold disc of farr behind the head, always dimmer than the painting's central light. He always faces the viewer's left.
