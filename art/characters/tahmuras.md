# Tahmuras · تهمورس

Hushang's son, called the div-binder. King in 04.01. Young enough at his accession to be his father's heir, already a man: the beard and the diadem are his from the first painting of that reign. He dies at the end of 04.01.

First painted in: 04.01 (`art/prompts/04-01.md`).

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

Tahmuras, Hushang's son, the div-binder, at miniature scale. The house face made a man's: dark almond eyes, a long straight nose, a firmer jaw than Hushang, a small closed mouth. A short dark beard, squared at the chin, not the three long white locks of Kayumars. Dark hair to the shoulders, held by a thin gold fillet. A narrow gold diadem, a simple band with one small plain gold disc, worn over the fillet; never a tall crown, never a turban. An indigo robe with a thin turquoise hem, a saffron sash, ivory trousers, and a narrow leopard-skin shoulder piece, pale tawny with black ringed rosettes, only on the left shoulder, not a full mantle. A heavy plain gold mace with a smooth ox-head and no engraving, at his side except when he lifts it. A soft pale-gold disc of farr behind the head, always dimmer than the painting's central light. He always faces the viewer's left.
