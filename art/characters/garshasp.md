# Garshasp · گرشاسپ

Treasurer of Fereydun's court and commander of Manuchehr's left wing. Not Salm, though both can wear green: Garshasp's cloth is turquoise, Salm's robe is malachite.

First painted in: 07.14 (`art/prompts/07-14.md`).

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

Garshasp the treasurer and champion, a mature man at miniature scale. A long face, dark eyes, a short black beard with grey at the chin. A soft turquoise cap at court. In battle, a plain iron helmet with one turquoise disc, and the turquoise cloth kept as a short cloak. Turquoise cloak over ivory and fine ring-mail, a heavy gold torque. No crown and no farr. He always faces the viewer's left.
