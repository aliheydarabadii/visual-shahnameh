## Prompt line

Fereydun's other elder brother, a young man at miniature scale, called the precious one. The same oval face as Kiyanush, with rounder cheeks and a thinner dark beard. Dark hair to the shoulders, a thin gold fillet with no disc and no plume. No crown and no farr. An ivory tunic with a turquoise hem, and no cloak. He always faces the viewer's left.
