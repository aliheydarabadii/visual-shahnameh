# Qobad the scout · قباد طلایه

The vanguard captain of Manuchehr's army in 07.15, and the scout who carries a challenge in the battle that follows. He is not Kay Qobad, the later king. Do not reuse this prompt line for that king.

First painted in: 07.15 (`art/prompts/07-15.md`).

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

Qobad the vanguard scout of Manuchehr's army, not the later king Kay Qobad. A young man at miniature scale, slimmer than the champions. Oval face, a thin black mustache, no beard, dark eyes. A light iron cap with no plume. A short lapis cloak over a thin mail shirt and ivory trousers. A short spear with a plain steel head. No crown and no farr. He always faces the viewer's left.
