# Golrang · گلرنگ

Fereydun's rose-coloured horse. He carries Fereydun into the Arvand and on to Zahhak's palace.

First painted in: 06.06 (`art/prompts/06-06.md`).

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

Golrang, Fereydun's horse, at miniature scale. A rose-madder coat, not neon and not chestnut-brown, an ivory mane and tail, dark hooves, a gold bridle with three turquoise beads and no inscription. Flat manuscript horse, not a photographed animal. He faces the viewer's left, the same way as his rider.
