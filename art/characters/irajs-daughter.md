# Iraj's daughter · دختر ایرج، مادر منوچهر

Unnamed in the poem. Daughter of Iraj and Mah-Afarid. Fereydun betroths her to Pashang, and she gives birth to Manuchehr. The poem says she looks like Iraj from head to foot.

First painted in: 07.12 (`art/prompts/07-12.md`).

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

A young woman at miniature scale who looks like Iraj. Oval face, dark almond eyes, a long straight nose, a small closed mouth, cheeks a pale arghavan rose. Black hair like musk in two long locks, a pale ivory veil edged in turquoise worn back so the face is clear. A rose-lavender robe with a thin gold hem, modest and fully clothed, never nude. A narrow gold fillet with no disc. No crown, no farr, no wings. She always faces the viewer's left.
