# Siyamak · سیامک

Kayumars' son, killed by the black div in 02.01.

First painted in: 02.01 (`art/prompts/02-01.md`).

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

The same face as Kayumars, made young. No beard. The same almond eyes, the same long nose, a smoother jaw. Dark hair in two long locks past the shoulders, held by a thin gold fillet with no disc. At court, a hip-length leopard mantle of the same pale tawny and the same black ringed rosettes, over an ivory tunic. In battle the chest is bare and the leopard skin sits only on the shoulders; when he falls, that skin is torn at the waist. Slender. He always faces the viewer's left.
