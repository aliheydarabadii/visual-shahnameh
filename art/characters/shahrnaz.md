## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

Jamshid's elder daughter at miniature scale. Fair ivory skin, an oval face, arched dark brows, dark almond eyes, a long straight nose, a small mouth. A tiny dark beauty mark on the visible cheek. Black hair in one long braid ending in a plain gold tube. A saffron veil over a turquoise robe with a narrow vermilion sash. No crown and no diadem. She always faces the viewer's left.
