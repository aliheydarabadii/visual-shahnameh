# Barmayeh · برمایه

The cow who nurses Fereydun. Peacock-coloured, the finest of her kind. Zahhak has her killed at the end of 06.03; 06.04 remembers her.

First painted in: 06.03 (`art/prompts/06-03.md`).

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

A cow at flat manuscript scale, not a living animal and not a heraldic bull. Slender. An ivory ground coat patterned all over with small peacock-eye feathers in turquoise, malachite, saffron and a very little vermilion. Two short plain gold horns curving forward, with no inscription. A small pale-gold star on the brow, always dimmer than the painting's central light. One dark almond eye, outlined, calm, not a human face. No wings, no halo, no cloth. Alive, she stands. Struck down, she lies with legs folded and head lowered, the pattern unchanged, and with no blood.
