# Envoy of the East · فرستاده‌ی خاور

The pure-hearted, eloquent man Salm and Tur choose from their assembly when they hear that Manuchehr has been crowned (07.14). He is not the mobed envoy of 07.06 (`mobed-envoy.md`), not a king, and not the dehqan. The poem does not name him.

First painted in: 07.14 (`art/prompts/07-14.md`).

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

The eloquent envoy Salm and Tur choose in the East after Manuchehr is crowned. Not the mobed of an earlier embassy, and not a king. A man of about thirty-five at miniature scale. Calm oval face, neat black beard, dark intelligent eyes, no grey. An olive-brown travel coat, a cream soft cap with no disc and no plume, ivory trousers, a dusty hem. A cylindrical case patterned only with a geometric star, never with letters. No staff, no diadem, no farr. He always faces the viewer's left.
