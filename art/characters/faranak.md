# Faranak · فرانک

Fereydun's mother. Young in 06.03; the same woman sixteen years later in 06.04 and 06.05.

First painted in: 06.03 (`art/prompts/06-03.md`).

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

A woman at miniature scale, mother of Fereydun. Oval face, high forehead, dark almond eyes, a long straight nose, a small closed mouth. Dark hair in two braids under a sheer ivory veil edged with a thin malachite border. A deep indigo robe with a narrow gold hem and ivory undersleeves. No crown, no farr, no jewels in the hair. Small. She always faces the viewer's left.
