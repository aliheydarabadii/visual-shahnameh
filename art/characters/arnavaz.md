Jamshid's younger daughter at miniature scale. Fair ivory skin, a rounder face than her sister, the same arched dark brows, the same long straight nose, dark almond eyes, a small mouth, and no beauty mark. Black hair loose under a shell-pink veil. An ivory robe with a malachite hem and one thin gold necklace with a single turquoise drop. No crown and no diadem. She always faces the viewer's left.
