# Sarv · سرو

King of Yemen, father of the three veiled daughters. The poem names him Sarv. He faces the viewer's right so the Iranian envoys, who face left, meet him. His house is palms, coral plaster and a wrapped headcloth, not the Kayanid cap.

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

Sarv, king of Yemen, a compact middle-aged man at miniature scale. Warmer brown pigment than the Iranian court, flat, not photoreal. A rounder face, a short dense black beard, dark eyes, a broad nose. A tall wrapped white headcloth bound with a gold cord and one small plain round gold disc, never a Kayanid cap and never a European crown. A deep coral-vermilion robe with ivory arabesque and a turquoise border. Small gold earrings. He always faces the viewer's right.
