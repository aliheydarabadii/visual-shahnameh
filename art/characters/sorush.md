# Sorush · سروش

Messenger pari who warns Kayumars and later urges revenge. Recurs in later chapters.

First painted in: 02.01 (`art/prompts/02-01.md`).

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

Not an angel of religious art, and not a divine person. A slender pari: an oval calm face, dark eyes, no beard, no crown, no halo. A short leopard cloak, paler tawny than the king's, with the same black ringed rosettes. Two narrow gold wings drawn as arabesque leaves, not bird feathers. No weapon. Small.
