# Tur · تور

Middle son of Fereydun, by Shahrnaz. Unnamed when he first appears in 07.02. Heavier in the shoulder than Salm or Iraj. The saffron robe and vermilion sash are his colours into the Turan years.

First painted in: 07.02 (`art/prompts/07-02.md`).

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

The middle son of Fereydun, a youth at miniature scale, heavier in the shoulder than his brothers and still small in the frame. The same long narrow face, high forehead, dark almond eyes, long straight nose and small closed mouth. A short square dark beard. Dark hair to the jaw under a thin gold fillet with one small vermilion bead. A saffron robe, a vermilion sash, a gold belt, ivory trousers. No crown. A sheathed straight sword with a dark hilt. A soft pale-gold disc of farr behind the head, always dimmer than the painting's central light. He always faces the viewer's left.
