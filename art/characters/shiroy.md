# Shiroy · شیروی

Champion in Manuchehr's host, called the lion-taker. Not a king. Leaner than Qaren, and not Garshasp. No lion skin.

First painted in: 07.17 (`art/prompts/07-17.md`).

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

A champion at miniature scale, not a king. A long face, dark almond eyes, a black beard forked in two points and no third lock. No crown, no fillet, no farr. A steel helmet wrapped in a malachite cloth. A malachite cloak over a short silver mail net, ivory trousers. A straight sword with a dark hilt and a short spear. Leaner than Qaren. He always faces the viewer's left. No lion skin.
