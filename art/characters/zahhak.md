# Zahhak · ضحاک

Mardas's son, called Bivarasp. Prince, then the serpent-shouldered king of the Arabs and of Iran.

First painted in: 05.02 (`art/prompts/05-02.md`).

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

A tall prince of the Arabs at miniature scale. Warm olive skin, a long narrow face, high cheekbones, heavy-lidded dark almond eyes, a long straight nose, a small closed mouth. Black hair to the shoulders, held by a narrow gold fillet with one oval garnet. Before he is crowned, no beard. After he is crowned, a short neat black beard along the jaw only, never a full beard and never white. A vermilion robe with a gold hem under a black cloak lined in saffron. One plain gold arm-ring on the right wrist. He always faces the viewer's right. Before the serpents, both shoulders are bare of snakes. After they grow, two small flat black serpents rise from the two shoulders, heads turned inward toward his neck, each eye a tiny vermilion dot, no wings and no scales painted as armour.
