# Abtin · آبتین

Fereydun's father. A young man of Tahmuras's line. Killed by Zahhak's order in 06.03; afterwards only a memory.

First painted in: 06.03 (`art/prompts/06-03.md`).

## Prompt line

A young man at miniature scale, father of Fereydun, of the same house. Oval face, high forehead, dark almond eyes, a long straight nose, a small firm mouth. A short dark beard, thinner than a king's. Dark hair to the shoulders, a thin gold fillet with no disc and no plume. No crown and no farr. An ivory tunic, a malachite belt, a plain brown traveling cloak. He always faces the viewer's left.
