# Qaren · قارن

Kaveh's son, called Qaren Kavgan. Commander of Manuchehr's host, elephant-bodied, the broadest man in the army. Not Kaveh, and not the bearer of the ox-headed mace.

First painted in: 07.14 (`art/prompts/07-14.md`).

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

Kaveh's son, the army's commander, at miniature scale but the broadest man in the host, elephant-bodied and still small in the frame. Square jaw, thick dark eyebrows, deep-set dark eyes, a short dense black beard cut square across the jaw, a little grey only at the temples. Short dark hair under a plain iron helmet with one small round gold boss and a mail aventail. Indigo tunic, fine ring-mail, ivory trousers, a dark leather belt. A heavy plain steel mace with a round head, no animal and no inscription. No crown, no fillet, no farr. He always faces the viewer's left.
