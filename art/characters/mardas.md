# Mardas · مرداس

Zahhak's father. A just Arab king. Dies in the garden pit in 05.02.

First painted in: 05.02 (`art/prompts/05-02.md`).

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears.

An old Arab king at miniature scale. Warm olive skin, a broad kind face, deep-set dark eyes, a long nose, a small closed mouth. A full silver-white beard, rounded and unpointed, to the chest, never divided into three locks. A white linen headcloth held by a narrow gold band with one turquoise stone at the front. Never a tall crown, never a leopard skin, never a farr disc. An ivory robe with a malachite hem under a dark indigo cloak. Soft ivory shoes, except when he washes, when the feet are bare. He always faces the viewer's left.
