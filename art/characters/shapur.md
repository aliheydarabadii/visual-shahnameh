# Shapur · شاپور

The champion who leads Salm's envoy in, and who binds the kettledrum on the war-elephant. Not a Sasanian king.

First painted in: 07.14 (`art/prompts/07-14.md`).

Shapur the champion, at miniature scale, the elephant-rider. A round face, dark eyes, a short rounded black beard. An iron helmet with one plain ivory oval boss. An indigo coat, a narrow turquoise sash, ring-mail, ivory trousers. He is shown with a small flat manuscript war-elephant. No crown and no farr. He always faces the viewer's left.
