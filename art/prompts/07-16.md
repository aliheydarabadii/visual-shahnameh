# 07.16 · نبرد منوچهر با سلم و تور

Seventy-eight couplets. The battle Manuchehr was raised for. News reaches Tur and Salm; Qobad carries an insult and brings it back; Manuchehr laughs, swears by the sun and the moon, and the host vows in the night. At dawn the plain flowers with tulips. Tur and Salm plan a night raid. Manuchehr hides, rises from the rocks, and takes Tur with a spear. He turns back to his own lamps.

07.15 ended with the Iranian army drawn up like a bride, drums and war-lions. This threshold is the empty plain between the hosts, one watch-fire at the centre. Each centre is the doorway into the next painting: fire, dust, the spoken spark, the banner, the lamp, the oath-light, the torch, the diadem's spark, the tent lamp, the dawn, the meeting spark, the plotters' lamp, the banner in the ambush, the sword-stroke, the spear's point.

Character sheets are law. The prompt line is pasted unchanged. Age notes from the sheet follow the line and do not replace it. The divine is never a figure. No letters in any script. Wrap is already in each prompt below.
