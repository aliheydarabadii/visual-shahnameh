# 06.05 · ضحاک → داستان کاوه آهنگر با ضحاک: illustration prompts

Eighty-six couplets. Zahhak, afraid, has a false testimony written. Kaveh the smith comes for his son, refuses to sign, tears the sheet, and raises his leather apron on a spear. The market follows him to Fereydun, who dresses the leather as the Kaviani banner. Fereydun takes leave of his mother, speaks with his brothers Kiyanush and Shadkam, and has the ox-headed mace made.
