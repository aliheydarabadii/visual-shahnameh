# 06.02 · خواب دیدن ضحاک → illustration prompts

Sixty-four couplets. Forty years of the reign remain. Zahhak dreams that
three warriors come from the palace: two elders and one younger between
them, tall as a cypress, with the ox-headed mace. The younger nooses him and
drags him to Damavand. He wakes screaming. Arnavaz asks, and he tells her.
She sends him to gather the mobads. At dawn he asks them who will end the
reign. For three days no one speaks. On the fourth he threatens them. One
mobad answers: all kings die; the one who comes is Fereydun, not yet born;
he will grow, lift the ox-mace, and hate Zahhak because Zahhak will kill
his father and the cow Barmayeh. Zahhak faints, then searches the world and
cannot rest. The bright day turns lapis on him.

The threshold is the bedchamber before sleep, the lamp still whole. The
dream's young king is the future Fereydun: paste `art/characters/fereydun.md`
unchanged and add the ox-headed mace. He is not a child here. The two elders
beside him are unnamed. They are not Salm or Tur. Do not use their sheets.

Zahhak always has the short black beard and both serpents. Arnavaz keeps
her sheet. The unnamed mobad is described below and is not a separate file.

Each centre is the doorway into the next. Miniature figures. Central 45%.
No text, no frame, no gore. No face or hand in the sky. No depiction of
God, the Prophet or the Imams.

## Figures

Paste `zahhak`, `arnavaz`, `fereydun`, and where a vision shows them,
`abtin` and `barmayeh`.

**Zahhak:** short black beard, both serpents, garnet fillet.

**Fereydun in the dream and in the prophecy:** the prompt line as written,
crown and short beard included. Add: a steel mace with a flat stylized
buffalo head, no inscription.

**The bold mobad** (this section only): An old mobad, miniature scale. A
long iron-grey beard, a narrow face, deep-set eyes, a brown robe, a plain
white turban with no jewel and no inscription. No crown. He always faces
the viewer's left. He is not Mardas and not Iblis.

**Two dream elders:** miniature, grey beards, plain indigo coats, no crowns,
smaller than Fereydun, facing the viewer's left. Unnamed. Not Salm or Tur.

### 00-khabgah: threshold

No people.

SUBJECT: A royal bedchamber at night. A low bed with an ivory cover and a
shell-pink cushion. A gold lamp on a stand beside the bed, burning steadily.
Slim columns recede into lapis, suggesting a hundred-column house without
any numeral. One cypress seen through a dark arch. No people, no serpents,
no dream yet. Carpets with geometric flowers only.
COMPOSITION: The lamp flame is at the exact centre and is the only bright
thing. Bed and lamp stay inside the central 45% width. Full-bleed, no border.
