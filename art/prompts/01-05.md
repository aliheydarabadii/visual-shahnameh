# 01.05 · آغاز کتاب → گفتار اندر آفرینش آفتاب: illustration prompts

Five couplets on the making of the sun. 01.04 ended on the still centre of the turning dome, a point of light with a faint ruby core. That point becomes the sun: a blue wheel made of red ruby, hung with lamps like a Nowruz garden, then a jewel travelling a perfectly straight road from east to west, then a sun whose rays stop short of the one who asks it to shine. There is no face on the sun, ever. The withheld light is the door to the moon in 01.06.

The divine is never a figure. Wrap every SUBJECT and COMPOSITION in the template below. Files go in `art/originals/01-05/`. Do not paint a light layer.

### 00-yaghut: threshold

SUBJECT: Before the sun is a sun. A deep lapis field with one faint thin gold ring, the blue wheel. At the centre a dark red ruby disc, oval like a cabochon jewel, not a face and not a ball of fire. Inside the ruby a small intense point of white-gold light is just waking. A few tiny gold rosette stars, much dimmer. No rays yet, no landscape, no person. Paper grain visible. The ruby is the subject.
COMPOSITION: The waking point inside the ruby is at the exact centre of the frame and is the brightest element. The ruby itself is centred. Dark lapis runs full-bleed to every edge. No vignette, no circular medallion border, no text.

### 01-nowruz: ز یاقوت سرخست چرخ کبود / بیاراسته چون به نوروز باغ

SUBJECT: The blue wheel adorned. The ruby disc has become a glowing red-gold sun with no face, no features, only mineral light. Around it, many small gold lamps, each lamp like a blossom, hanging in the lapis as if the night sky were a Nowruz garden. In the lower part, a narrow band of night garden: white blossoms, a few vermilion tulips, turquoise leaves, all dimmer than the sun. No people, no banners with writing, no numerals. Shell gold and lapis, vermilion only in the tulips and the ruby's heart.
COMPOSITION: The sun disc is at the exact centre and is decisively the brightest element, brighter than every lamp. Lamps stay mostly inside the central half. The garden band is along the bottom and may run to the edges as scenery. Full-bleed, no frame, no inscription.

### 02-gowhar: روان اندرو گوهر دلفروز / ز خاور برآید سوی باختر

SUBJECT: A heart-kindling jewel travelling. A perfectly straight horizontal band of pale gold light crosses the whole lapis field, the straightest road there is, from east to west. On that band, at the centre, one faceted white-gold jewel, brighter than the band, with a small ruby heart. No face. The eastern side of the sky is a slightly warmer lapis; the western side is deeper blue. Both are skies, not maps, and neither contains letters, birds as writing, or a horizon city. A few dim gold rosettes above and below the band.
COMPOSITION: The jewel is at the exact centre of the frame and is the brightest point. The gold band is perfectly horizontal through the centre, edge to edge. Nothing else competes. Full-bleed, no border, no text.

### 03-natabi: ایا آنکه تو آفتابی همی / چه بودت که بر من نتابی همی

SUBJECT: A sun that does not reach. A round red-gold sun disc with no face sits in the lapis, its rays a clean circle of shell-gold that stops abruptly. Just outside that circle, in the lower part, one tiny anonymous person in a plain blue robe and white turban stands in cooler shadow, small as a miniature figure, no halo, no wings, not a prophet and not a king. The rays do not touch the figure. The rest is night sky and a thin strip of dark ground. No writing, no second figure.
COMPOSITION: The sun disc is at the exact centre of the frame and is the brightest element. The ray-circle stays inside the central half. The tiny person is on the centre line in the lower quarter, still inside the central 45% width, and is clearly darker than the sun. Full-bleed, no frame.
