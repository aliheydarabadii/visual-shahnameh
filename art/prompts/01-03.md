# 01.03 · آغاز کتاب → گفتار اندر آفرینش جهان: illustration prompts

Twenty-four couplets on the making of the world. 01.02 ended in a silence too
bright to praise. This section opens that silence as the void, then goes from
nothing to the four elements, to the transient inn of the world, to the
turning dome of spheres, and down to an earth that burns like a lamp and a
garden that cannot walk. The divine is never a figure: only light, order and
absence. Each painting's centre is the doorway to the next. The last garden
is empty of animals and people, the door toward 01.04.

Wrap every SUBJECT and COMPOSITION in the template from `art/STYLE.md`.
The files go in `art/originals/01-03/`. Reuse `public/art/01-01/layer-abr.webp`
as the light layer; do not paint a new one.

### 00-hich: threshold

SUBJECT: Before creation. A vast empty lapis night with no ground, no
architecture, no figure and no moon. A single small intense point of
white-gold light at the centre, like a thing not yet made. A few tiny gold
rosette stars, sparse and much dimmer than the centre. Visible paper grain.
The emptiness is the subject.
COMPOSITION: The point of light is at the exact centre of the frame and is
the only bright thing. No vignette frame, no border, no circular medallion
edge. The dark field runs full-bleed to the edges.

### 01-hakim: حکیما چو کس نیست گفتن چه سود / به گیتی بپوی و به هر کس بگوی

SUBJECT: A tiny old sage, white beard, blue robe, white turban, walks a pale
path through towering stacked spongy rocks in lapis, rose, ochre and
malachite, at night. He carries a small lamp. The path climbs toward a gap
between the peaks filled with soft gold light, the beginning he is going to
tell. Along the path, two closed books and one open book lie on the rock;
pages show only faint geometric ornament, never letters. Small plane trees.
COMPOSITION: The glowing gap is at the exact centre of the frame and is the
brightest element. The sage is small, in the lower third, on the centre line.
The path leads from the bottom centre up to that gap.

### 02-az-hich: که یزدان ز ناچیز چیز آفرید

SUBJECT: Thing being drawn out of nothing, with nobody present. A dark lapis
field. At the centre a seed of white-gold light is just opening, and four
faint threads of colour begin to leave it: saffron, turquoise, pale ivory, and
earth-brown. They are not yet fire, water, wind and earth, only the start of
order. No hand, no face, no body, no angel, no divine figure. Gold dust
drifts. It should feel like absence becoming a thing.
COMPOSITION: The seed is at the exact centre and is the brightest point, much
brighter than the four threads. The threads stay inside the central half.
Dark lapis at the corners. Full-bleed, no medallion frame.

### 03-chahar: یکی آتشی برشده تابناک / میان آب و باد از بر تیره خاک

SUBJECT: The four elements in one flat vertical stack, manuscript cosmology,
not a diagram with labels. At the top, wind as pale gold-white chi cloud
ribbons. In the middle, a tall almond flame of white-gold fire. Around the
flame, a horizontal band of turquoise water. At the bottom, dark earth: dry
cracked ochre on one side, moist malachite on the other. No animals, no
people, no letters, no numerals.
COMPOSITION: The flame is at the exact centre of the frame and is decisively
the brightest element. The stack occupies the central 45% width and runs
nearly the full height. Water sits on either side of the flame, wind above
it, earth below it.

### 04-sepanji: ز بهر سپنجی سرای آمدند

SUBJECT: A Persian caravanserai at night, the world as a transient inn, seen
straight on across a square court. Four iwans, one toward each side. No
inscriptions anywhere on the tilework. In the four iwans, one element each,
as a motif: a small flame, a water basin, a curl of gold cloud, a dark bed of
soil. Four young cypresses rise around the court like necks lifted. A single
gold lamp hangs in the open court. No people, or at most one tiny sleeping
figure far from the lamp, not at the centre.
COMPOSITION: The hanging lamp is at the exact centre of the frame and is the
brightest point. The court is symmetrical. Tile ornament is geometric and
floral only. Full-bleed; the building runs off the edges, no outer frame.

### 05-gonbad: پدید آمد این گنبد تیزرو / فلکها یک اندر دگر بسته شد

SUBJECT: The swift dome as an illuminated cosmological diagram on deep lapis,
cousin to an astrolabe page, with a sense of slow rotation. Concentric gold
rings. On one ring, twelve identical small gold rosette stars, evenly spaced,
standing for the twelve signs, with no glyphs, no animals and no numerals. On
the inner rings, seven planetary discs, all dimmer than the centre: a gold
sun, a silver crescent, a bright eight-pointed star, a dim pale-blue star
with a thin ring, and three smaller gold discs. From the centre, quiet rays
of white-gold light pour outward. Nobody is there; the light itself is the
bestowing. No numbers, no letters.
COMPOSITION: A small intense point of white-gold light is at the exact centre
and is the brightest element. The rings are centred on that point and run
past the edges of the frame. Planets must not outshine the centre.

### 06-cheragh-zamin: زمین شد به کردار روشن چراغ

SUBJECT: A night landscape in stacked miniature space. Distant spongy
mountains in lapis and mauve, a dark strip of sea, a meadow, a spring just
breaking from rock. At the centre a low mound of earth glows from within like
a lamp, white-gold light inside the soil. One large gold star hangs above
that mound and casts its light down into the glow. A few young shoots rise
from the lit soil. No people, no animals, no text.
COMPOSITION: The glow inside the earth is at the exact centre of the frame
and is the brightest point, brighter than the star above it. Mountains and
sea stay in the upper half. The meadow fills the lower half. Key forms inside
the central 45% width.

### 07-derakht: همی گشت گرد زمین آفتاب / گیا رست با چند گونه درخت

SUBJECT: A still night garden that cannot walk. A great plane tree stands
rooted at the centre, its branches weighed down by fruit and blossom. Among
the leaves a small gold sun disc sits in a white-gold opening of sky, and a
thin gold arc around that opening shows the sun turning. To one side of the
trunk a small flame rises; on the other a thin spring falls. Both are dimmer
than the centre. No animals and no people: the garden is empty, waiting.
Malachite, turquoise water, vermilion only as a few small fruits.
COMPOSITION: The white-gold opening with the sun is at the exact centre of
the frame and is the brightest element. The tree fills the middle. Flame and
spring stay inside the central half. Dark lapis sky in the corners. Full-bleed.
