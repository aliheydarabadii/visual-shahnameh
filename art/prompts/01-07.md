# 01.07 · آغاز کتاب → گفتار اندر ستایش پیغمبر: illustration prompts

Thirty-two couplets in praise of the Prophet, the companions and the household. 01.06 ended on a point of light between the moon and the sun. That light comes down onto the sea.

Show the Prophet, Ali, Fatimah, the companions and God only as light, veils, the sea and ships. Ferdowsi's own image: the world is a sea, a hard wind raises the waves, seventy ships are built, one broad ship is adorned like a bride, and the household is in it. Never paint them as figures, faces, silhouettes, angels or riders. No halos on human bodies. No names, no Arabic, no calligraphy. A tiny anonymous seeker, seen from behind, is allowed only where a prompt says so, and that seeker is not the Prophet.

The divine is never a figure. Wrap every SUBJECT and COMPOSITION in the template below. Files go in `art/originals/01-07/`. Do not paint a light layer.

### 00-darya: threshold

SUBJECT: A still dark sea under lapis night. No shore, no boat, no person. At the centre, one small point of white-gold light resting on the water, with a quiet reflection downward. A thin veil of gold mist lies across the water, not a solid curtain. A few gold rosette stars, dim. The emptiness and the one light are the subject. Paper grain.
COMPOSITION: The point of light is at the exact centre of the frame and is the only bright thing. Sea and sky meet in a high horizon above the centre. Water runs full-bleed to the side and bottom edges. No frame, no medallion, no text.

### 01-ab: به گفتار پیغمبرت راه جوی / دل از تیرگیها بدین آب شوی

SUBJECT: A night garden and a stream, no people. Dark lapis banks. A stream of pale gold water runs into a round pool that shines white-gold, as if the water itself were washing the dark away. Beside the stream, dimmer than the pool: one small unlit oil lamp and one open book whose pages show only a geometric star, never letters. A few white flowers. No figure, no angel, no name.
COMPOSITION: The shining pool is at the exact centre and is the brightest element. The stream arrives from the lower centre. Book and lamp sit just beside the pool, inside the central 45% width. Garden runs to the edges. Full-bleed, no frame, no writing.

### 02-chahar-cheragh: که خورشید بعد از رسولان مه / بیاراست گیتی چو باغ بهار

SUBJECT: An empty spring garden at night, no people anywhere. A red-gold sun disc with no face hangs as pure light. Around it, exactly four gold lamps on four slender posts, symmetrical, each lamp a small flame, standing for lights and not for bodies. The garden is in blossom: white flowers, turquoise leaves, a few vermilion tulips. No banners, no inscriptions, no tile writing, no names, no faces in the sun or the lamps.
COMPOSITION: The sun disc is at the exact centre of the frame and is brighter than all four lamps. The four lamps stand close around it, inside the central 45% width, two slightly above and two slightly below, or in a square, but none of them at the exact centre. Blossoms may continue to the edges as scenery. Full-bleed, no border.

### 03-dar: که من شهر علمم علیم در ست

SUBJECT: A city of knowledge with nobody in it. Flat miniature domes and walls in lapis, turquoise and sand, seen straight on. A tall pointed arch gate stands open. Through the gate there is only white-gold light, with no figure, no silhouette and no face in the doorway. Gold dust drifts in the air like a voice made visible, abstract specks, never letters. Tilework is geometric and floral only, with no inscription anywhere. No flags with marks.
COMPOSITION: The white-gold light inside the gateway is at the exact centre of the frame and is the brightest element. The arch is symmetrical and centred. The city spreads to the left and right edges and is dimmer than the gate. Full-bleed, no outer frame, no text.

### 04-mah: نبی آفتاب و صحابان چو ماه / به هم بسته‌ی یکدگر راست راه

SUBJECT: A red-gold sun disc and a silver moon disc, neither with a face, bound by one perfectly straight gold path. Where the path joins them, a small hard knot of white-gold light. No people, no horses, no names. Below the path, a faint scatter of gold dust on dark ground, not a footprint in the shape of a body. Deep lapis around.
COMPOSITION: The knot of light is at the exact centre and is brighter than both discs. The sun and the moon sit on the centre line to either side, inside the central half, close enough to read as bound together. The gold path is horizontal through the centre. Full-bleed, no frame, no text.

### 05-kashti: چو هفتاد کشتی برو ساخته / یکی پهن کشتی بسان عروس

SUBJECT: The world as a sea, in flat miniature space, high horizon, no Western perspective. A hard wind: gold chi cloud bands, sails filled. Many small ships with raised white and gold sails, scattered from the foreground to the horizon, uncounted, with no numeral anywhere and no people on the decks. One great ship at the centre, broader than the rest, hung with garlands of flowers. It is adorned like a bride only in the sense of ornament: no woman, no face, no body. On its prow a round jewelled ornament like an eye, geometric, gold and a little vermilion, not a face. A lantern on the great ship. Waves in stacked bands of lapis and turquoise.
COMPOSITION: The lantern on the great ship is at the exact centre of the frame and is the brightest element. The great ship occupies the central 45% width. The many smaller ships are scenery toward the edges and are dimmer. Full-bleed, no border, no text, no figure.

### 06-arus: محمد بدو اندرون با علی / کرانه نه پیدا و بن ناپدید

SUBJECT: Close on the great ship, which fills the middle. Its deck is hidden by veils of gold and lapis cloth. Behind a parted veil there is only white-gold light, no body, no face, no silhouette of a person. The sea around the ship has no shore and no bottom: dark water to every edge, a few waves. Far away, on a low rock at the bottom edge, one tiny wise figure seen from behind, plain robe, no halo, small as a seed, looking toward the ship. He is not the Prophet and must not look like one: no light pours from him.
COMPOSITION: The light behind the parted veil is at the exact centre and is the brightest element. Veils stay inside the central half. The tiny figure is at the bottom edge, not at the centre, and is dark. Water full-bleed. No frame, no text, no faces.

### 07-chashme: خداوند تاج و لوا و سریر / همان چشمه‌ی شیر و ماء معین

SUBJECT: An empty throne of ivory and gold, with a crown set on the seat and nobody sitting, no body, no face, no angel beside it. A gold banner hangs from a pole with no emblem, no animal and no letter. Four springs rise in a row of small basins: a deep red spring (wine), a saffron spring (honey), a white spring (milk), and a clear turquoise spring. No people drinking. Paradise as objects and water only.
COMPOSITION: The clear turquoise spring is at the exact centre of the frame and is the brightest element, a white-gold clarity in the water. Throne and crown sit just above it, banner to one side, the other three springs close around, all inside the central 45% width, all dimmer than the clear spring. Dark garden behind, full-bleed, no frame, no text.

### 08-khak: برین زادم و هم برین بگذرم / چنان دان که خاک پی حیدرم

SUBJECT: A road of fine gold dust through a dark lapis landscape, no people. The dust itself is the subject. One mark in the dust like a single footprint made of light, abstract and small, not attached to a leg or a body. The road leads to a small white-gold light. A side path splits off into darker ground and is not lit. A few stones, one small cypress far off. No face, no name, no shrine with writing.
COMPOSITION: The white-gold light at the end of the road is at the exact centre and is the brightest element. The road runs from the bottom centre up to that light. The footprint is on the road, inside the central 45% width, dimmer than the light. The dark side path stays secondary. Full-bleed, no frame, no text.

### 09-atash: هر آنکس که در جانش بغض علیست

SUBJECT: A dark quiet sea. Far off, the same ship's lantern reduced to one small steady white-gold point. To one side, far from the centre and clearly less bright, a small contained vermilion fire on a distant shore, with no body in it, no crowd, no weapons. The fire is a sign seen from far away, not a spectacle. The sea is calm. No faces, no script, no figure.
COMPOSITION: The lantern-point is at the exact centre of the frame and is the brightest element. The fire is well off the centre, smaller, and must not compete. Dark water and sky full-bleed. No frame, no text.

### 10-bagh: بر باغ دانش همه رفته‌اند

SUBJECT: A garden of knowledge at night, empty of people. Plane trees and cypresses, a pale path, flowers. Along the path, many tiny separate motes of gold, like lights that have already walked there, not people and not letters. An open book lies on the path with only a geometric pattern on its pages. The path leads into an opening of white-gold light that has no far edge, a garden with no shore. No building sign, no inscription.
COMPOSITION: The opening of light is at the exact centre of the frame and is the brightest element. Trees frame it and may reach the edges. The book and the motes stay inside the central half, dimmer than the opening. Full-bleed, no border, no text.
