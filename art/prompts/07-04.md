# 07.04 · رفتن پسران فریدون نزد شاه یمن

Twenty-four couplets. The three sons leave Fereydun's house at dawn, ride to Yemen, and are met with saffron, musk and dinars. Sarv gives them his three daughters, speaks his bitterness that he has no son, then says the gift aloud before the mobeds. Howdahs turn back toward Fereydun.

This follows 07.03, which ends with the sons leaving Fereydun full of his counsel. The threshold is the night they sleep at home. Each centre is the doorway into the next painting. Character lines come from `art/characters/` and are pasted unchanged, with one age sentence after the line.

The divine is never a figure. No text in any script. Full-bleed, no frame. Files: `art/incoming/07-04/`.
