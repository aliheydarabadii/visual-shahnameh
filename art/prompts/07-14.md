# 07.14 · فریدون → فریدون

One hundred and twenty-eight couplets. Salm and Tur, in the East, hear that
Manuchehr has the throne. They load elephants and send an eloquent envoy to
buy pardon. Fereydun receives him under a lamp, then answers that the sun
cannot be hidden and that Iraj's coffin is still narrow. The envoy rides back
like the wind. His description of the court makes the East afraid, and the
two armies set out.

The camera moves east tent → treasury → road → Iran's lamp → the coffin →
the banner → the road home → the vision of the court told aloud → the march.
Each centre is the doorway into the next painting. Character prompt lines are
pasted from `art/characters/` unchanged, with only the dated age notes.
