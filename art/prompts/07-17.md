# 07.17 · نامه منوچهر به نزد فریدون

One hundred and thirty-two couplets. Manuchehr writes to Fereydun: three days of war, the ambush, Tur's death. A camel carries the letter and a wrapped head. Fereydun weeps and blesses him. Salm runs for a fortress in the sea-cloud. Qaren takes Tur's ring, opens the gate by a lie, and Shiroy storms it. Salm finds no boat. Manuchehr on the dun horse Charme overtakes him. The remnant asks mercy. He calls it the day of justice and puts the weapons down. Treasure, elephants and turquoise thrones come to Tamish. He kisses the ground. Fereydun kisses his face, turns to a faceless heaven, and seats him on the gold throne.

07.16 ended at the camp lamps after Tur's fall. This threshold is the empty tent and the unopened letter. The centres move from that lamp, through the seal, the fortress window, the open gate, the fire, the empty water, the sword, the faceless sky, and the cup given from the throne.
