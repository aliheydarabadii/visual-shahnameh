# 01.01 · آغاز کتاب → آغاز کتاب: illustration prompts

Fifteen couplets praising the Creator of soul and wisdom, who is beyond name,
place and thought. The journey goes **upward and inward**: from a threshold on
earth, past a lamp of wisdom, out through the turning heavens, to a last veil
that thought cannot pass. Each painting's centre is the doorway to the next.

### 00-dargah: threshold

SUBJECT: A monumental Persian iwan portal (pishtaq) at night, seen straight
on and symmetrical. Its tall pointed arch is lined with lapis and turquoise
tilework, gold arabesques and a muqarnas half-dome. Through the arch there is
only a deep, softly glowing lapis night with a few gold rosette stars. Two
slender cypress trees flank the portal. Gold cloud bands drift across the
lower foreground.
COMPOSITION: Perfect bilateral symmetry. The dark arch opening is at the exact
centre and holds a faint central glow. The portal fills about 70% of the
height.

### 01-cheragh: به نام خداوند جان و خرد

SUBJECT: A single small oil lamp of gold and turquoise on a carved stone
ledge at the summit of stacked spongy mountains (lapis, mauve, ochre). Its
flame is a tall almond of white-gold light, with fine gold threads of light
radiating out into a deep lapis night sky full of small gold rosette stars.
Gold chi cloud bands curl around the flame without touching it.
COMPOSITION: The flame is at the exact centre and is the brightest point in
the painting. Mountains rise from the lower third. The sky is vast and calm.

### 02-sepehr: خداوند کیوان و گردان سپهر / فروزنده ماه و ناهید و مهر

SUBJECT: The turning heavens drawn as an illuminated cosmological diagram,
like an astrolabe in a manuscript of wonders. Concentric gold rings of the
spheres sit on deep lapis, sprinkled with gold rosette stars. Along the rings
are a gold sun disc with flame-like rays, a silver-white crescent moon,
Venus as a bright eight-pointed star, and Saturn as a dim, cool, pale-blue
star with a thin ring. Fine arabesque scrolls connect the rings. There is a
sense of slow rotation.
COMPOSITION: Rings centred on the frame, with a small, intense point of
white-gold light at the exact centre. The rings extend past the edges.

### 03-negarande: ز نام و نشان و گمان برترست / نگارنده‌ی بر شده پیکرست

SUBJECT: The painted vault of heaven. Billowing chi cloud bands in gold,
turquoise and ivory part like curtains around a central opening of pure,
blinding gold-white light. Inside the clouds, faint gold arabesque patterns
are being drawn as if by an unseen brush. Nobody is present. It feels like
something too bright to look at.
COMPOSITION: A tunnel of clouds converging on the centre, which is the
brightest gold-white. Darker lapis at the corners.

### 04-tarazu: خرد را و جان را همی سنجد اوی / در اندیشه‌ی سخته کی گنجد اوی

SUBJECT: A scholar's night pavilion, seen in the flat miniature manner with a
tiled floor, a carpet and an open window onto a starry lapis sky. On a low
lectern lies an open manuscript (its pages show only abstract marks, no
legible letters). Beside it, a delicate gold balance scale hangs perfectly
still with empty pans. Through the window, a vast field of stars that the
scale clearly cannot weigh.
COMPOSITION: The window with its stars is at the exact centre and gives the
brightest light. The lectern and scale are in the lower middle. Symmetrical
architecture.

### 05-rah: پرستنده باشی و جوینده راه

SUBJECT: A tiny traveller in a blue robe and white turban, with a staff,
walking a thin winding path that zigzags up through towering stacked spongy
rocks in lapis, rose, ochre and malachite, with small plane trees and a
stream. At the top of the path is a gap between the peaks, filled with soft
gold dawn light.
COMPOSITION: The path leads the eye in an S-curve from bottom centre up to
the glowing gap at the exact centre. The traveller is small, in the lower
third, near the centre line.

### 06-pir-borna: توانا بود هر که دانا بود / ز دانش دل پیر برنا بود

SUBJECT: An old sage with a long white beard sits cross-legged under a great
plane tree (chenar), reading a book. The half of the tree behind him is
winter-bare, and the half above the book bursts into spring blossom and
young green leaves, as if the reading makes it bloom. Small birds, a stream
and flowering shrubs. Night, with a gold moonlit glow, under a lapis sky.
COMPOSITION: The open book glows softly at the exact centre, the source of
the light. The tree's canopy fills the upper half.

### 07-parde: از این پرده برتر سخن‌گاه نیست / ز هستی مر اندیشه را راه نیست

SUBJECT: A vast embroidered curtain, deep night-blue silk worked with gold
arabesques and stars, hangs across the whole world, very slightly parted at
the centre. Through the narrow parting comes pure white-gold light, so bright
that nothing behind it can be seen. Gold dust drifts in the light. Below it is
a dark, calm floor of lapis.
COMPOSITION: The parting is at the exact centre: a thin vertical slit of
light. The curtain fills the frame. Strong symmetry, solemn and still.

### layer-abr: gold cloud bands (light layer)

SUBJECT: Gold Chinese-style chi cloud bands from Persian manuscript
illumination: ribbon-like, curling and luminous, with fine line detail. Paint
them only in gold and white-gold on a pure black (#000000) background.
COMPOSITION: The clouds frame the four edges and corners; the centre 50% of
the frame is completely empty black. Nothing else in the frame. Landscape 3:2.
