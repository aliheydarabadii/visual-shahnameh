# 01.06 · آغاز کتاب → گفتار اندر آفرینش ماه: illustration prompts

Eight couplets on the moon. 01.05 ended on a sun whose rays stopped short. This section is the night that follows: a lamp prepared for the dark, the thin yellow crescent like a back bent by love, the full moon at two weeks, and at last the thinning moon drawing near the sun, held in one unchanging nature. No face is ever drawn on the moon or the sun. The meeting-light at the end is the door to the praise in 01.07.

### 00-shab: threshold

SUBJECT: The night the sun withdrew. A vast lapis field. At the very bottom edge only, a thin warm gold line, the sun already gone. At the centre a pale silver disc, the moon as a lamp not yet kindled: the disc is quiet silver, but its core is a small clearly luminous white point so the eye has a light to enter. A few tiny gold rosette stars, dimmer than that core. No face on the disc, no landscape, no person, no numeral.
COMPOSITION: The luminous core of the silver disc is at the exact centre of the frame and is the brightest element. The disc is centred. Dark lapis runs full-bleed to the edges. No border, no medallion frame, no text.

### 01-cheragh: چراغست مر تیره شب را بسیچ

SUBJECT: The moon as a lamp for the dark night. A full silver-gold moon, no face, glowing like a hung lamp. Around it a ring of many small identical gold dots, evenly spaced, with no numerals and no glyphs. Below, a dark miniature landscape just receiving the light: a few flat roofs, one cypress, a strip of meadow, all small and dim. The world is still mostly night. No people, no writing on the roofs.
COMPOSITION: The moon is at the exact centre and is decisively the brightest element, brighter than the ring of dots. The landscape occupies the lower fifth and may run to the side edges as scenery. Dots stay near the moon, inside the central half. Full-bleed, no frame, no text.

### 02-gom: پدید آید آنگاه باریک و زرد / چو پشت کسی کو غم عشق خورد

SUBJECT: A very thin yellow crescent, saffron and pale gold, no face, its two tips dissolving into the lapis so it seems about to vanish. The crescent is curved like a bent back. In the lower part, one tiny anonymous figure in a plain muted robe is bent forward, echoing that curve, small, no halo, no wings, not a sacred person, face turned down and barely indicated in miniature fashion. Nothing else: no second figure, no letters, no heart symbols.
COMPOSITION: The crescent is at the exact centre of the frame and is the brightest element, even though the whole painting is dim and yellow rather than white. The bent figure is on the centre line in the lower quarter, inside the central 45% width, and is darker than the crescent. Dark lapis to every edge. No frame.

### 03-do-hafte: به دو هفته گردد تمام و درست / بدان باز گردد که بود از نخست

SUBJECT: The moon returning to itself. A large full silver-gold moon, no face, at the centre. Along a shallow gold arc, a sequence of smaller moon discs showing the change: thin crescent, wider crescent, half, gibbous, then the full moon, then back toward thin. All the smaller discs are dimmer than the full moon. No numerals, no labels, no faces on any disc. A quiet dark meadow at the bottom, no people.
COMPOSITION: The full moon is at the exact centre and is the brightest element. The arc of smaller discs stays inside the central 60% and must not contain a disc brighter than the centre. Meadow along the bottom edge. Full-bleed, no frame, no text.

### 04-nahad: بود هر شبانگاه باریکتر / بدینسان نهادش خداوند داد

SUBJECT: The ordained meeting, with no divine person and no faces. A thin saffron crescent on one side and a red-gold sun disc on the other, both inside the middle of the picture, both dimmer than the centre. Between them, at the exact centre, a small intense point of white-gold light where their lights join. That point is the one unchanging nature. No hand, no angel, no face in either disc, no script. Deep lapis around them, a few gold rosettes.
COMPOSITION: The white-gold point is at the exact centre of the frame and is decisively brighter than both the sun and the crescent. Sun and crescent sit left and right of it, inside the central 45% width. Full-bleed, no border, no text.
