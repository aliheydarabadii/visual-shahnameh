# 01.10 · آغاز کتاب → ستایش انوشه روان امیر منصور: illustration prompts

Sixteen couplets in praise of the dead patron, Amir Mansur. When the poet set
his hand to the book there was a young hero, wise and modest, who asked what
he needed and promised to meet it himself. The poet held him like a fresh
apple, and from low dust rose as far as Saturn. Then that cypress was lost
from the garden: neither alive nor any sign of the dead, gone to man-killing
whales. The heart trembles like a willow and remembers his counsel, to turn
from crookedness toward justice. His last charge: if you speak this book of
kings, give it to the kings.

The journey: the apple in a still garden, the young amir beside a lamp, the
apple offered and no wind, Saturn above the ascent, an empty circle where the
cypress stood, a willow and a straightened path, the book set on a road of
light toward a distant crown.

Amir Mansur must not be confused with Sultan Mahmud, who appears only as a
distant empty crown in the last painting. Mansur is young, with a low diadem,
never a tall sun-crown, never a turquoise imperial throne.

- Amir Mansur: a young prince of about twenty-five, a light brown short beard,
  a low narrow gold diadem and not a tall crown, a shell-gold robe patterned
  with small malachite leaves, a wide gold belt with a round turquoise buckle,
  vermilion boots. A kind, quiet face. Small in the frame.
- The poet: a small man of about forty, slender, olive skin, a long narrow
  black beard with two silver streaks at the chin, a plain white turban, a
  deep indigo robe with a thin gold hem and a turquoise cuff, ivory slippers.
  No crown.

### 00-sib: threshold

SUBJECT: A night garden, still, with no wind. On a low gold dish sits one
fresh apple, ivory and pale gold with a small vermilion blush, luminous as
if lit from inside. Behind it a young straight cypress, darker than the
apple. A still pool, plane trees at the sides, gold chi clouds low, a lapis
sky with a few gold rosette stars. No person.
COMPOSITION: The apple is the brightest point at the exact centre. The
cypress rises on the centre line behind it and does not outshine it. Quiet
symmetry, full-bleed.

### 01-amir: جوان بود و از گوهر پهلوان / خداوند رای و خداوند شرم

SUBJECT: A private night room, modest, not an imperial court. Amir Mansur, a
young prince of about twenty-five, light brown short beard, a low narrow gold
diadem and not a tall crown, a shell-gold robe patterned with small malachite
leaves, a wide gold belt with a round turquoise buckle, vermilion boots, sits
on a low cushioned seat. His face is kind and quiet. Opposite him sits the
poet: about forty, long narrow black beard with two silver streaks, plain
white turban, deep indigo robe with a thin gold hem, a closed geometric book
beside him. Between them a slender gold lamp. Tiled walls, one carpet, a
window of stars. No text.
COMPOSITION: The lamp flame is the brightest point at the exact centre. Both
figures are small and flank it, the amir slightly higher. No second light. He
must not wear a tall sun-crown and must not sit on a high turquoise throne.

### 02-dast: مرا گفت کز من چه باید همی / همی داشتم چون یکی تازه سیب

SUBJECT: The same Amir Mansur, low gold diadem, malachite-leaf robe, turquoise
belt-buckle, extends an open hand. The same poet, indigo robe and white
turban, sits with open hands, receiving. Between them on the carpet: one fresh
apple on a gold dish, glowing, a small pile of gold coins, and a closed book
with a geometric cover and no letters. Through the window the cypresses are
perfectly still, no wind. Night, lapis and gold.
COMPOSITION: The apple is the brightest point at the exact centre. The open
hand and the coins sit just around it and are dimmer. Figures are small.
Stillness is the subject.

### 03-keyvan: به کیوان رسیدم ز خاک نژند / به چشمش همان خاک و هم سیم و زر

SUBJECT: A vertical ascent in flat miniature space. Dark earth at the bottom,
a thin gold path rising. The poet, small, indigo robe and white turban, is
on the path. At the exact centre hangs Saturn: a pale cool blue-white star
with one thin gold ring, luminous. In the lower third, three equal small
bowls hold dark dust, silver leaves and gold coins, none of them brighter
than the star. At the side of the path, only Amir Mansur's open hand, the
same young prince's hand with a malachite-leaf cuff, small, the generosity
that lifts. No throne. Lapis night, gold rosette stars.
COMPOSITION: Saturn is the brightest point at the exact centre. The path
leads up to it. The three bowls are equal and dim. The poet is small on the
path, below the star.

### 04-sarv: چنان نامور گم شد از انجمن / به دست نهنگان مردم کشان

SUBJECT: A night garden. A ring of trees, and one empty circle of dark soil
where a tall cypress stood. In that circle there is no body and no ghost with
a face: only a faint vertical thread of gold light, an absence. On the soil
lies his gold belt with the round turquoise buckle, unlit compared with the
thread. Beyond a low wall, dark water holds two stylized miniature
crocodiles, lamp-black and dull green, small, no blood and no victim. The
cushions of an assembly are empty. No corpse, no angel.
COMPOSITION: The faint gold thread in the empty circle is the brightest point
at the exact centre, narrow and vertical. The belt lies just below it. The
crocodiles stay low and to the side, darker. Mournful and still.

### 05-bid: گرفتار زو دل شده ناامید / ز کژی روان سوی داد آوریم

SUBJECT: A night riverbank. A willow, its thin branches drawn as if trembling.
Beneath it the poet, small, indigo robe, white turban, silver-streaked beard,
hands closed. On the ground a path that was crooked becomes one straight gold
line, and the line ends at a simple oil lamp, the counsel remembered. No
judge, no angel, no halo. Lapis water, gold chi clouds, spongy rocks.
COMPOSITION: The oil lamp is the brightest point at the exact centre. The
willow fills the upper half and is darker. The poet is small, just below the
centre line. The gold path leads into the lamp.

### 06-nameh-shah: مرا گفت کاین نامه‌ی شهریار / بدین نامه من دست بردم فراز

SUBJECT: A long tiled iwan at night. The poet, both hands, indigo robe and
white turban, sets a closed book onto a path of light. The book's cover is a
gold geometric medallion with no letters, beginning to glow. The young amir is
absent. Far at the end of the iwan, small, an empty turquoise throne and a
tall sun-crown rest in a lesser light: a promise of the king of kings, with
nobody seated and no divine figure. Cypresses beyond.
COMPOSITION: The book's gold medallion is the brightest point at the exact
centre. The distant crown is smaller and dimmer, a doorway beyond the book.
The poet is small, lower centre. The iwan is symmetrical.
