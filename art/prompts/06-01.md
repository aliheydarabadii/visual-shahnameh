# 06.01 · پادشاهی ضحاک → illustration prompts

Forty-one couplets. A thousand years of Zahhak. Wisdom hides, sorcery is
honoured, and the divs' hands are long. Jamshid's two daughters, Shahrnaz
and Arnavaz, are brought trembling into the serpent king's hall and taught
crookedness. Each night two young men are killed and their brains fed to the
snakes. Armayil and Garmayil enter the kitchen, mix a sheep's brain with one
victim's, and send the other into the hills. Of those spared, the poem says,
comes a free people of the pasture. The king still calls warriors to wrestle
and takes girls from the curtain as servants.

The threshold follows 05.02's tired lamp. The lamp is now inside a vast dark
ivan: the same gold flame, a thousand years later. Zahhak in this section
always has the short black beard and the two serpents. He never wears
Jamshid's diadem.

Each centre is the doorway into the next painting. People stay miniature.
Key content stays inside the central 45% width. No text, no frame, no gore,
no depiction of God, the Prophet or the Imams.

Wrap every prompt in the template from `art/STYLE.md`. Paste each named
character's prompt line unchanged, then the EXTRA sentence.

### 00-eyvan: threshold

No people.

SUBJECT: A vast palace ivan at night, a thousand years deep. Rows of slim
columns painted flat, receding, lapis and dull gold, no inscriptions. At the
centre a low throne, empty, with a black cloak lined in saffron folded on
it. In front of the throne a single gold lamp. High on the back of the
throne, two small serpent shapes are only shadows woven in the cloth, not
living snakes. No people. The hall runs off every edge.
COMPOSITION: The lamp flame is at the exact centre and is the only bright
thing. Throne and lamp stay inside the central 45% width. Columns fade into
dark at the sides. Full-bleed, no border.

### 01-hezar: the long dark

PASTE: zahhak
EXTRA: Short black beard. Both serpents present.

SUBJECT: Zahhak on the throne, small, serpents at his shoulders, under a
heavy lapis dark. Around him, tiny courtiers: some hide books whose pages
show only geometric stars, faces turned down; one small figure in a tall
odd cap performs a sorcery, a dull green flame in his palm, dimmer than the
central lamp. No readable marks. The good are in shadow. The king is not
glorious. A gold lamp before the throne.
COMPOSITION: The lamp flame is at the exact centre and is brighter than the
green flame and the serpent eyes. Zahhak stays inside the central 45% width.
Full-bleed, no border.

### 02-dokhtaran: the two sisters

PASTE: zahhak
EXTRA: Short black beard. Both serpents present.
PASTE: shahrnaz
PASTE: arnavaz

SUBJECT: Two young women, Shahrnaz and Arnavaz, stand close together facing
left, small, veils as in their sheets, trembling, hands gathered. They have
just been led in. Zahhak sits to the right, facing right, serpents visible,
looking toward them. A gold lamp between the sisters and the king. No
chains. The hall is dark. Their faces are calm and recognisable, not
weeping rivers. No third woman.
COMPOSITION: The lamp flame is at the exact centre and is the brightest
point. All three faces stay inside the central 45% width. Full-bleed, no
border.

### 03-mardom: the nightly two

PASTE: zahhak
EXTRA: Short black beard. Both serpents present. He is small, deep in the hall.

SUBJECT: Night. Two young men in plain ivory tunics, beardless, no crowns,
are led by two guards toward a dark kitchen door. They are small, faces
lowered, not wounded, no blood. Beyond the door a faint gold light and the
tiny shape of Zahhak's two serpents. The kitchen lamp seen through the door
is the hope and the dread. No corpse.
COMPOSITION: The kitchen lamp seen through the doorway is at the exact
centre and is the brightest point. The two youths stay inside the central
45% width, in front of the door. Full-bleed, no border.

### 04-raz: the two cooks confer

PASTE: armayil
PASTE: garmayil

SUBJECT: A quiet corner at night, away from the king. Armayil and Garmayil
sit on a bench, facing left, heads inclined toward one another. A very small
lamp between them. Their aprons are off; they wear the malachite and
turquoise coats. No Zahhak. No victims. The mood is a whispered plan. A
closed door behind them.
COMPOSITION: The little lamp is at the exact centre and is the brightest

### 05-kelid: the kitchen is theirs

PASTE: armayil
PASTE: garmayil

SUBJECT: The palace kitchen at night. Armayil and Garmayil now in white
aprons, learning the dishes: a few plain bowls, a ladle, a bunch of herbs,
no labels. A large key hangs on a nail, dull. They work with their backs
half turned to a distant doorway where a guard is tiny. A kitchen lamp.
No king, no blood.
COMPOSITION: The kitchen lamp is at the exact centre and is the brightest
point. Both cooks and the lamp stay inside the central 45% width. Full-bleed,
no border.

### 06-shab: the night they cannot save both

PASTE: armayil
PASTE: garmayil

SUBJECT: Two youths in ivory have been brought to the kitchen floor, alive,
kneeling, unhurt, faces down. Armayil and Garmayil stand over them, aprons
on, looking at each other in grief, hands not yet raised to strike. A lamp
between the cooks. No blood, no knife in a wound, no brain. The horror is
the choice, not a killing shown. Dark kitchen.
COMPOSITION: The lamp flame is at the exact centre and is the brightest
point. The four figures stay inside the central 45% width and are small.
Full-bleed, no border.

### 07-zanhar: one is spared

PASTE: armayil
PASTE: garmayil

SUBJECT: The kitchen's back door opens onto night hills. Garmayil guides
one living youth out toward the dark, a hand on his shoulder, no rope.
Armayil, inside, mixes a dish: a sheep's head is whole and closed on the
table, not opened, beside a covered pot. No gore, no exposed brain. A lamp
on the threshold between the kitchen and the hill. The spared youth looks
back once.
COMPOSITION: The lamp on the threshold is at the exact centre and is the
brightest point. The doorway, the youth and the two cooks stay inside the
central 45% width. Full-bleed, no border.

### 08-dasht: the pasture

No portraits from the palace.

SUBJECT: An open night pasture. Small shepherds in plain ivory and brown,
no crowns, no serpents, keep goats and sheep. A few black tents. One gold
lamp outside the nearest tent. The people are young and calm. Distant
mountains. This is the life that grew from the spared. No palace, no king,
no battle.
COMPOSITION: The tent lamp is at the exact centre and is the brightest
point. The nearest tent and shepherd stay inside the central 45% width.
Flocks may drift to the sides, dimmer. Full-bleed, no border.

### 09-koshti: the king's sport

PASTE: zahhak
EXTRA: Short black beard. Both serpents present. He wrestles, so the cloak is off the shoulders but the vermilion robe is belted; the serpents remain.
PASTE: shahrnaz

SUBJECT: A palace court at night. Zahhak wrestles a young warrior in a plain
ivory tunic. Both are small, manuscript-flat, no blood, a hold not a wound.
The two serpents stay at Zahhak's shoulders. To one side Shahrnaz stands
with her saffron veil, now among servants, facing left, small and still.
A gold lamp hung between the wrestlers. No crowd large enough to crowd the
centre. Arnavaz is not in this painting.
COMPOSITION: The hanging lamp is at the exact centre and is the brightest
point, brighter than the garnet and the serpent eyes. Wrestlers and lamp
stay inside the central 45% width. Full-bleed, no border.
