# 07.05 · بخش کردن فریدون جهان را بر پسران

Thirteen couplets. Fereydun brings the secret out and divides the world in three. Salm receives Rum and the West, Tur receives Turan and China, Iraj receives Iran and the ivory throne. The three sit at peace.

This follows 07.04, which ends with the young men on the road back to Fereydun. The threshold is the night garden before the division is spoken. Each centre is the doorway into the next painting. The sons wear the plain disc diadem only from this section on.
