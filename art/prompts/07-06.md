# 07.06 · رشک بردن سلم، بر ایرج

One hundred and eleven couplets. A long time passes. Fereydun grows old. Salm, lord of the West, rejects the division and sends to Tur. Tur rages. The brothers meet, mix honey with poison, and send a mobed with a harsh message: the crown must leave Iraj, or east and west will bring maces against Iran. The envoy reaches a palace lost in cloud, sees the white-haired king, and speaks. Fereydun answers that the division was made with the wise and with fear of God, that greed has taken their wisdom, and that his own time to leave has come. The envoy kisses the ground and goes like the wind.

This follows 07.05, which ends with the three brothers seated in peace. The threshold is that peace gone dusty. Fereydun is the old king throughout. Salm and Tur are the later kings. Iraj is not shown in person except as a tiny ivory figure inside someone else's speech. God is never a figure: the oath is sun, moon and Venus as plain roundels.

No text. Full-bleed, no frame. Files: `art/incoming/07-06/`.
