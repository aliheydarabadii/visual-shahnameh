# 01.02 · آغاز کتاب → گفتار اندر ستایش خرد: illustration prompts

Fourteen couplets in praise of wisdom. 01.01 ended on a veil parted by light.
This section steps through that light into a place where wisdom can be spoken,
then follows what wisdom does: it guides across both worlds, holds joy and
sorrow, is lost, becomes the eye of the soul, keeps the three watches, and
at last is too large to praise. Each painting's centre is the doorway to the
next. The last light is silence, which 01.03 will open as the void.

Wrap every SUBJECT and COMPOSITION in the template from `art/STYLE.md`.
The files go in `art/originals/01-02/`. Reuse `public/art/01-01/layer-abr.webp`

### 00-hayat: threshold

SUBJECT: A quiet Persian courtyard at night, just beyond a parted curtain, seen
straight on in flat miniature space. A round pool of still lapis water sits in
the middle of a tiled court. In the pool is one soft white-gold reflection, as
if a lamp were about to be lit: the light the reader just came through. Two
slender cypresses stand on each side, kept in the inner half of the frame.
Gold chi cloud bands drift low across the foreground. No people.
COMPOSITION: Perfect bilateral symmetry. The white-gold reflection in the pool
is at the exact centre of the frame and is the brightest point. The pool
occupies the middle. Dark lapis night above. Full-bleed, no frame around the
picture.

### 01-majlis: کنون ای خردمند وصف خرد / کنون تا چه داری بیار از خرد

SUBJECT: A small night gathering on a carpet in that same courtyard. Three
miniature figures in robes and turbans, small in the frame, sit in a shallow
arc and lean inward to listen. At the centre a low gold oil lamp burns with a
tall almond of white-gold flame. Beside the lamp an open book lies on a low
stand; its pages are blank ivory with only a faint nonlinguistic geometric
ornament, never letters. A plane tree at each side. Lapis night, a few gold
rosette stars, gold chi clouds.
COMPOSITION: The flame is at the exact centre and is the brightest point.
Figures are small and sit in the lower middle, inside the central half. The
book does not compete with the flame.

### 02-do-saray: خرد رهنمای و خرد دلگشای / خرد دست گیرد به هر دو سرای

SUBJECT: Two worlds stacked in flat miniature space, joined by wisdom. The
lower half is an earthly night garden: cypress, a thin stream, a pale path.
The upper half is the other world, a calm field of gold rosette stars on deep
lapis. A tiny traveller in a blue robe and white turban stands on the path and
reaches upward. A ribbon of white-gold light, not a hand and not a figure,
comes down from the star field and meets his hand. No face, no body and no
divine figure anywhere in the light.
COMPOSITION: The meeting of the ribbon and the hand is a point of white-gold
light at the exact centre of the frame, and it is the brightest element. The
traveller is small, in the lower third, on the centre line. The two worlds
fill the frame edge to edge.

### 03-shadi-gham: ازو شادمانی وزویت غمیست / خرد تیره و مرد روشن روان

SUBJECT: One night garden, not a poster split. A narrow channel of white-gold
light runs vertically through the middle like a lit stream. The bank on the
viewer's left is spring: malachite leaves, saffron blossoms, a small lit lamp,
increase. The bank on the viewer's right is winter: bare branches, a darkened
lamp, mauve spongy rock, decrease. A tiny bright-faced figure in a pale robe
stands on the winter bank, small, not smiling, turned toward the lit channel.
He is a man whose soul is bright while wisdom is dark.
COMPOSITION: The lit channel crosses the exact centre, and the brightest point
is a white-gold gleam in the water at the exact centre of the frame. Both
banks stay beautiful. The figure is small, in the lower right of the centre
half, dimmer than the water.

### 04-rish: کسی کو خرد را ندارد ز پیش / هشیوار دیوانه خواند ورا

SUBJECT: A closed garden gate at night, seen straight on, set in a lapis wall
with turquoise tile and gold arabesque, and no inscription. In the middle of
the gate is a round opening onto a warm gathering: a glow of lamps beyond,
figures only as tiny silhouettes if at all. Outside the gate, in the lower
middle, a tiny solitary figure in a dim robe stands with head bowed, holding a
torn vermilion rose, the heart wounded by his own deeds. He is shut out of
the light. Spongy rocks and one dark cypress at the sides.
COMPOSITION: The round opening of warm light is at the exact centre of the
frame and is the brightest element, the way back to wisdom. The shut-out
figure is small, below centre, and does not outshine the opening.

### 05-chashm: خرد چشم جانست چون بنگری

SUBJECT: Not a giant eye and not a face. An almond-shaped window, a chashm,
cut in a deep lapis wall ornamented with gold eslimi. Through the almond is a
field of gold rosette stars, the other world seen from this one. A single
white-gold star burns in the middle of that opening: wisdom as the eye of the
soul. On the tiled floor below, a tiny traveller steps toward the window. A
small closed gold fetter lies on the tiles beside his path, the bound foot of
severed wisdom, not worn as a costume centrepiece.
COMPOSITION: The white-gold star is at the exact centre of the frame and is
the brightest point. The almond window sits in the central 45% width. The
figure and the fetter are small, in the lower middle.

### 06-se-pas: سه پاس تو چشم است وگوش و زبان

SUBJECT: A night wall of three small arched niches around a guardian lamp.
No letters on the wall. In the centre niche a gold lamp burns with a tall
white-gold flame: wisdom, first of creation, guardian of the soul. The upper
left niche holds a small round turquoise mirror, the eye. The upper right
niche holds a curved gold rosewater ewer, the ear, a listening vessel. Just
beneath the lamp, a small ivory cup with no inscription, speech, the tongue.
Nothing is a face, an eyeball or a mouth.
COMPOSITION: The flame is at the exact centre of the frame and is decisively
the brightest element. The three objects are smaller and dimmer, inside the
central half. Strong near-symmetry. Dark lapis at the corners.

### 07-setayesh: خرد را و جان را که یارد ستود

SUBJECT: The same carpet as the gathering, now empty. Cushions unoccupied,
books closed, their covers plain, pages unseen. Where the lamp stood, there
is no flame and nobody: a still flood of white-gold light, too bright to
speak into, with a little gold dust drifting in it. The room is otherwise
dark lapis. It feels like praise that has become silence.
COMPOSITION: The white-gold light is a soft disc at the exact centre and is
the brightest element, brighter than anything else in the book of this
section. Empty carpet below. Dark corners. Full-bleed, solemn, no frame.
