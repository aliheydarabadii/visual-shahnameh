# 01.04 · آغاز کتاب → گفتار اندر آفرینش مردمان: illustration prompts

Twenty-one couplets. 01.03 ended on a plane tree with the sun held in its branches, an empty garden waiting. This section comes down out of that sun into the meadow, where beasts appear, then humankind standing straight as a cypress, then the question of what a person is, and last the swift dome that does not wear out. The dome's still centre is the door to the sun in 01.05.

The divine is never a figure: only light, order and absence. No angels. Each painting's brightest point is the doorway to the next. People, when present, are tiny manuscript figures, never portraits and never sacred persons.

### 00-marghzar: threshold

SUBJECT: We have come through the sun that sat in the plane tree's branches, and we are now in the night meadow at the tree's roots. The lower trunk and spreading roots of a great plane tree frame the top of the picture, with a few white blossoms and two small vermilion fruits. The meadow is malachite and turquoise grasses and small ivory flowers, empty of animals and people. On the grass, a round pool of white-gold light, as if the sun had fallen there and was waiting. Deep lapis night in the corners. No moon with a face.
COMPOSITION: The pool of white-gold light is at the exact centre of the frame and is the only bright thing. The trunk touches the top edge; the meadow runs to the bottom and side edges. No circular medallion, no border. Full-bleed.

### 01-jonbande: وزان پس چو جنبنده آمد پدید / نه گویا زبان و نه جویا خرد

SUBJECT: The same night meadow, now inhabited by small manuscript animals and no people. A deer lowers its head to drink, a horse sleeps with folded legs, a small humped ox grazes, a hoopoe perches. They are miniature, flat, and small, with patterned coats, not realistic wildlife. Plants are under them: grasses, a low shrub. The pool is now still water holding a white-gold reflection. No speech, no books, no tools. A few gold chi cloud bands in the lapis sky, dim.
COMPOSITION: The bright reflection in the pool is at the exact centre and is brighter than every animal. Animals stay inside the central half, small, none of them at the centre. Meadow full-bleed to the edges. No text, no frame.

### 02-penhan: چنینست فرجام کار جهان / نداند کسی آشکار و نهان

SUBJECT: Nobody present. A single vertical slit of white-gold light, like a curtain not quite closed, dividing a revealed night garden on one side of the slit from a featureless deep lapis darkness on the other. The garden side shows only a little grass, one flower and a hint of the plane-tree root, dimmer than the slit. The dark side is empty. This is the end of the world's work, half known and half hidden. No hand drawing the curtain, no face, no angel.
COMPOSITION: The slit of light is exactly at the horizontal centre and its brightest point is at the exact centre of the frame. Garden and darkness stay close to the slit, inside the central half; the corners are dark lapis. Full-bleed, no medallion frame, no written inscription.

### 03-sarv: چو زین بگذری مردم آمد پدید / سرش راست بر شد چو سرو بلند

SUBJECT: A single tall dark-green cypress. Its tip opens into a white-gold flame of light. At the foot of the cypress stands one tiny human, upright, in a simple blue robe and white turban, about the height of a flower compared with the tree, echoing the cypress. No halo, no wings. Around that figure, even smaller, a deer, a horse and a bird face inward, obedient. Night meadow, a few flowers. No crowd, no weapons, no writing.
COMPOSITION: The white-gold light at the cypress tip is at the exact centre of the frame and is the brightest element. The cypress trunk drops from that light toward the lower centre. The tiny person and the animals are in the lower centre, inside the central 45% width. Sky at the corners. Full-bleed.

### 04-do-giti: ترا از دو گیتی برآورده‌اند / نخستین فطرت پسین شمار

SUBJECT: The two worlds meeting, with no divine person. The lower half is the night meadow. The upper half is a lapis sky with small gold rosette stars. Between them, one tiny upright human in a blue robe stands inside a soft almond of white-gold light. The light is not a saint's halo painted on the head; it is the place the two worlds join, and it is larger than the person. Below the person, a short vertical chain of three dim small forms: a stone, a seedling, a small deer, the intermediaries. No angels, no face in the sky, no script.
COMPOSITION: The white-gold almond is at the exact centre and is the brightest thing, brighter than the stars and the person. The chain of intermediaries sits just below centre, inside the central 45% width. The horizon of meadow and sky crosses behind the almond. Full-bleed, no border.

### 05-ranj: به رنج اندر آری تنت را رواست / سر اندر نیاری به دام بلا

SUBJECT: A tiny scholar, blue robe, white beard, bent over a small task at a low desk: tracing a geometric star pattern on a page. The page has only the geometric pattern, never letters. A single oil lamp of gold and turquoise burns. To one side, dim and clearly not entered, a dark loop of thorny vine lying on the ground like a snare. The work is lit; the snare is in shadow. No second person, no divine figure.
COMPOSITION: The lamp flame is at the exact centre of the frame and is the brightest element. The scholar is small, just below the lamp, inside the central 45% width. The snare stays inside that same central half but is dark. Desk and page close to the lamp. Dark lapis room or night garden behind, running to the edges. No frame, no writing.

### 06-gonbad: نگه کن بدین گنبد تیزگرد / نه از جنبش آرام گیرد همی

SUBJECT: The swift dome as an illuminated cosmological diagram on deep lapis, cousin to an astrolabe, slowly turning. Concentric gold rings run off the edges. No numerals, no zodiac animals, no glyphs. Inside the rings, two small dim motifs only: a turquoise drop (the cure) and a tiny vermilion thorn (the pain), both much dimmer than the centre. At the centre a still point of white-gold light with a faint warm ruby core, the first hint of the sun, perfectly still while the rings turn. Nobody is there.
COMPOSITION: The still point is at the exact centre of the frame and is decisively the brightest element. Rings are centred on it. The drop and the thorn sit close to the centre, inside the central 45% width, and must not outshine it. Full-bleed, no medallion border, no text.
