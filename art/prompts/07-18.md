# 07.18 · درگذشتن فریدون

Eleven couplets. The reign ends. The Kayanid tree fails. Fereydun steps down from crown and throne and sets before himself the heads of his three sons. He dies. His name and his justice remain. Manuchehr puts on the diadem and a blood-red sash. They build a tomb of red gold and lapis, hang the crown over an ivory throne, and seal the door. The world is a fable and a wind.

07.17 ended with Manuchehr on the gold throne and the treasure shared. This threshold is the same throne already empty, under a tree that is losing its leaves. The last doorway is the gold boss of the sealed door.

Character sheets are law. The prompt line is pasted unchanged. Age notes from the sheet follow the line and do not replace it. The divine is never a figure. No letters in any script. Wrap is already in each prompt below.
