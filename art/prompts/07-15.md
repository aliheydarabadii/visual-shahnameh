# 07.15 · فریدون → آهنگ رزم منوچهر با سلو و تور

Thirty-seven couplets. The eastern armies near Iran. Fereydun tells a parable
and sends Manuchehr from the mountain onto the plain. The Kaviani banner, the
elephants and the named champions array the host until it shines like a moon
and sounds like a kettledrum. The last jewel is the doorway into the battle.

Prompt lines are pasted unchanged. Manuchehr is armed only from the moment he
binds the mail. Sarv keeps facing right.

SUBJECT: An empty plain at night before any army moves. A grove of dark elms. A pale path. No people, no horses, no banner yet. One folded ivory cloth on the path, empty. Spongy rocks, lapis sky, dim rosette stars. A single distant white-gold point, like a jewel not yet raised.
