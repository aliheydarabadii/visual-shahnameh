# 01.08 · آغاز کتاب → گفتار اندر فراهم آمدن شاهنامه: illustration prompts

Seventeen couplets on how the book was gathered. A poet who cannot climb the
great tree builds a foundation at its foot. The poem is not a lie: part of it
agrees with wisdom, the rest carries meaning in cipher. An ancient book, once
scattered among the mobads, is sought out by a dehqan hero. He questions the
aged priests about the kings, and from their speech a memorial is set down.

The journey goes from a height no foot can reach, down to a lamp on a new
foundation, into the open page, out to the scattered pieces, to the seeker,
to the council, and at last into the book itself. Each centre is the doorway
to the next.

Recurring figures, described in full in every prompt so each painting stands
alone:

- The poet: a small miniature man of about forty, slender, olive skin, a long
  narrow black beard with two silver streaks at the chin, calm eyes, a plain
  white turban, a deep indigo robe with a thin gold hem and a turquoise cuff,
  ivory slippers. No crown.
- The dehqan: an older, broad-shouldered host, a full white beard, a felt
  ochre kolah with no jewel and no crown, a malachite-green coat and a saffron
  sash.

Wrap every SUBJECT and COMPOSITION in the template from `art/STYLE.md`.
Append to every prompt: "Output exactly 1536x1024 pixels. No divine or
religious figures, no God, no Prophet, no Ali, no angels, no halos. Books and
papers show only blank pages or nonlinguistic geometric ornaments, never
writing, letters or script."

### 00-sarv: threshold

SUBJECT: A single enormous cypress at night, so tall that its crown leaves
the top of the frame. The trunk is dark malachite and lamp-black; the foliage
is a dense spire. Inside the dark foliage, at the exact centre of the frame,
a small opening of soft white-gold light, a high place no foot can reach. The
ground is a quiet lapis court. A few gold chi cloud bands drift low. No
person, no building, no text.
COMPOSITION: The cypress stands on the centre line. The brightest point is
that small gold opening at the exact centre, framed by darker foliage. Still,
symmetrical, full-bleed. The sky is deep lapis with a few gold rosette stars.

### 01-paye: اگر بر درخت برومند جای نیابم / توانم مگر پایه‌ای ساختن

SUBJECT: The poet, a small miniature man of about forty, slender, olive skin,
a long narrow black beard with two silver streaks at the chin, a plain white
turban, a deep indigo robe with a thin gold hem and a turquoise cuff, ivory
slippers, no crown. He stands at the foot of a great cypress and lays a low
gold plinth of three steps against the trunk, a foundation. Beside the cypress
a tall date palm casts a calm shade. A small oil lamp of gold and turquoise
burns on the plinth. Stacked spongy rocks far behind, lapis night, gold
rosette stars. No writing.
COMPOSITION: The lamp flame is the brightest point and sits at the exact
centre. The poet is small, in the lower centre. The cypress trunk rises
behind the lamp. The palm stands to one side, inside the picture but not
brighter than the flame.

### 02-ramz: تو این را دروغ و فسانه مدان / ازو هر چه اندر خورد با خرد

SUBJECT: A night pavilion with a tiled floor and a starry window. On a low
lectern lies one open manuscript. One page is a clear field of pale gold with
a few simple geometric rosettes, wisdom that can be looked at and is not
writing. The other page is the same book veiled in a dense gold arabesque
cipher, meaning hidden in ornament. No letters, no words, no script of any
kind. The poet, small, about forty, long narrow black beard with two silver
streaks, white turban, deep indigo robe with a thin gold hem, stands to one
side looking, not writing.
COMPOSITION: The centre of the clear gold page is the brightest point and
sits at the exact centre of the frame. The cipher page is dimmer and off to
the side. Symmetrical architecture. The window's stars do not outshine the page.

### 03-parakande: یکی نامه بود از گه باستان / پراگنده در دست هر موبدی

SUBJECT: A night library court. Seven small aged mobads, white beards, pale
blue and ivory robes, white turbans, no halos and no religious symbols, stand
apart toward the edges. Each holds a different unbound quire or scroll. The
pages are blank or carry only geometric ornament, never script. In the middle
of the tiled floor a round pool of white-gold light marks the place where the
pieces have not yet been joined: an empty glowing circle. Gold dust turns in
the light. Cypresses beyond the court.
COMPOSITION: The empty circle of light is the brightest point at the exact
centre. The mobads and their fragments stay outside it, smaller toward the
edges. Flat miniature space, high horizon.

### 04-dehqan: یکی پهلوان بود دهقان نژاد / پژوهنده‌ی روزگار نخست

SUBJECT: The dehqan, an older broad-shouldered man with a full white beard, a
felt ochre kolah with no jewel and no crown, a malachite-green coat and a
saffron sash, sits on a carpet in a modest night pavilion. This is not a
throne room. Before him a low lamp and a stack of blank quires with geometric
ornament only, never letters. Through a pointed window directly behind the
lamp, a mist of tiny gold silhouettes: ancient riders, horses and cities, far
away, the first days he is seeking. He looks toward that light. No text.
COMPOSITION: The lamp flame is the brightest point at the exact centre, set
in the window so the flame is the doorway into the ancient world. The dehqan
is small, lower centre. The silhouettes are dimmer than the flame.

### 05-anjoman: ز هر کشوری موبدی سالخورد / بگفتند پیشش یکایک مهان

SUBJECT: A night assembly in a garden court. The same dehqan, full white
beard, felt ochre kolah, malachite coat, saffron sash, sits lower centre.
Around him in a shallow arc, seven small aged mobads with white beards and
pale robes, hands open as they speak. There is no fire-altar and no divine
symbol. A single gold brazier flame in the middle is only a lamp. Behind
them, faint and tiny, a dim frieze of miniature kings, horses and walled
cities, the world they are describing, kept darker than the flame. A pool,
cypresses, gold chi clouds. No text anywhere.
COMPOSITION: The brazier flame is the brightest point at the exact centre.
Figures are small and sit in the lower half. The frieze is a dim band, not a
second light.

### 06-nameh: چنین یادگاری شد اندر جهان

SUBJECT: The same dehqan, white beard, ochre kolah, malachite coat, is now
still. He sets a thick closed book onto a low gold stand. The cover is deep
lapis with a gold geometric medallion and no letters, and the medallion glows
from within. A tiny dark musk-pod sits beside the stand, small and not
bright. The mobads have fallen quiet in a ring, heads inclined. Through a
pointed arch beyond them, a calm night world of tiny towns and cypresses, as
if the memorial has entered the world. No script on the book or the arch.
COMPOSITION: The glowing medallion of the book is the brightest point at the
exact centre. The dehqan is small, just below it. Quiet symmetry. The musk-pod
and the towns are dimmer than the book.
