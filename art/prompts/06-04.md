Thirty-four couplets. Sixteen years after the fire, Fereydun comes down from Alborz and asks his mother who he is. She tells him Abtin, the line of Tahmuras, the serpents, the cow, the flight. He vows to go against Zahhak with a sword. She says he has no footing against the world, and wishes his days glad. Eight paintings.
