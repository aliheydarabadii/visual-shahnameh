# 03.01 · هوشنگ → پادشاهی هوشنگ: illustration prompts

Thirty-eight couplets. The reign of Hushang. He takes his grandfather's crown,
orders the seven climes, draws iron out of stone, meets a world-burning
serpent on the mountain, and from the two stones learns fire. He makes that
fire the qibla and names the night Sadeh. Then the saw and the adze, rivers
led out of the seas, seed and harvest, the tame herds, and the furs. He dies.
Only a good name remains. The world will not show its face.

This follows 02.01, which ended on Kayumars' empty throne. The threshold is
that seat the next night: the diadem waiting, nobody in it.

Hushang is the boy of 02.01, grown. Paste his prompt line unchanged, then the
grown-king sentence. Where they conflict, the grown-king sentence wins.
Kayumars is dead and does not appear. The divine is never a figure: prayer is
a fire and a lapis sky with no face, no hand and no writing. No depiction of
God, the Prophet or the Imams.

Each centre is the doorway into the next painting. The brightest point is
small, white-gold, and at the exact centre (pixel 768, 512). People stay
miniature. Everything that matters stays inside the central 45% of the width.
Full-bleed: no border, frame, margin or signature. No letters in any script,
no numerals, no inscriptions.

Wrap: the style paragraph below, then SUBJECT, then the character lines, then
COMPOSITION, then the closing lines. Files: `art/incoming/03-01/`.

## Style paragraph

Persian miniature painting, Timurid–Safavid manuscript style (Herat/Tabriz
school, 15th–16th century), opaque mineral pigments and shell gold on
burnished paper, fine brushwork, flat stacked perspective, no Western
shading. Night palette: deep lapis-lazuli and ultramarine, glowing gold leaf,
small vermilion accents, malachite and turquoise, ivory. Visible paper grain
and slight gold craquelure.

## Closing lines

Strictly no text, letters, calligraphy, numerals, border, frame, margin or
signature. Not a 3D render, not photographic, not fantasy concept art. No
depiction of God, the Prophet or the Imams. No face, no hand and no writing
in the sky. Landscape 3:2, 1536 by 1024. The single brightest point is a
small white-gold light at the exact centre of the frame.

## Characters

Paste both Hushang blocks, in this order, into every prompt where he appears.

**Hushang, prompt line (unchanged):** A boy of the same house, younger and smaller than Siyamak. Rounder cheeks, the same almond eyes, the same long nose, no beard. Dark hair cut to the jaw, held by the same thin gold fillet. Leopard skin worn as a short belted tunic, the same pale tawny and the same black ringed rosettes, with ivory trousers. A short knife with an ivory hilt at his belt, sheathed except when he strikes the div. He always faces the viewer's left.

**Hushang, grown king (03.01 note; this wins on age, beard and crown):** He is no longer a boy. A man's height at miniature scale. The same face, the same almond eyes, the same long nose, the same leopard colours, the same thin gold fillet. A short dark beard along the jaw. Once crowned, Kayumars' plain gold diadem over the fillet: a simple band with one small plain gold disc, never a tall crown. The ivory-hilt knife stays sheathed. He still always faces the viewer's left.

### 00-gah

No characters. No Hushang yet.

SUBJECT: The night after Kayumars. A low stone seat on a mountain, empty. A
leopard skin, pale tawny with black ringed rosettes, is folded on the seat
with no wearer. On the skin lies a narrow gold diadem, a simple band with one
small plain gold disc, and that disc is the only bright thing. A cave mouth
behind the seat is dark. Stacked spongy rocks in lapis, rose, ochre and
malachite run off every edge. One cypress. Dull gold chi clouds along the
lower edge. No people, no animals, no body, no second crown.
COMPOSITION: The diadem's plain disc is a small white-gold point at the exact
centre of the frame. The seat and the folded skin stay inside the central 45%
width. Rocks run off every edge. Full-bleed, no border.

### 01-taj

Characters: grown Hushang, both blocks. A few tiny followers in the same
leopard tunic, too small for faces to compete. No Kayumars.

SUBJECT: The new king on the mountain at night. Grown Hushang sits on the
low stone seat, crowned with the plain one-disc diadem, leopard tunic, short
dark beard, sheathed ivory-hilt knife. His pale-gold farr is a small disc
behind his head, dimmer than the centre. Around him, far back and dim, seven
small hills of different mineral colours stand for the seven climes. Two or
three tiny leopard-clad men sit lower on the rocks. A cypress and a younger
cypress. Quiet beasts, a ram and a deer, small, at the sides. It is still a
night painting.
COMPOSITION: A small white-gold point at the heart of the farr, just in front
of his chest, is at the exact centre and is the brightest element, brighter
than the diadem. Hushang is small and entirely inside the central 45% width.
The seven hills are dim and must not outshine the centre. Full-bleed, no
border.

### 02-ahan

Characters: grown Hushang, both blocks.

SUBJECT: The first iron. Night, among spongy rocks. Grown Hushang stands at a
low stone anvil. A split grey boulder lies open. From it he draws a short bar
of iron, and the middle of that bar is white-hot. A small furnace glow sits
behind the bar, dimmer than the iron. No letters on the metal. No modern
tools: only the stone and the glowing bar. The leopard tunic and the one-disc
diadem are unchanged. The knife stays sheathed.
COMPOSITION: The white-hot middle of the iron bar is a small white-gold point
at the exact centre and is the brightest element, brighter than the furnace.
Hushang is small, just below centre, inside the central 45% width. Full-bleed,
no border.

### 03-mar

Characters: grown Hushang, both blocks. The serpent is not a dragon.

SUBJECT: One day on the mountain. A long serpent, charcoal-black, wingless,
flat manuscript drawing, not a photograph and not a Western dragon. Two eyes
like small vermilion springs, not lamps. A wisp of lamp-black smoke from the
mouth, and the world near it a little darker. No horns, no legs, no crown.
Grown Hushang has stopped on the path, one rough stone raised in his hand.
The stone's edge catches a small white-gold glint. The serpent faces him and
does not yet strike. Night rocks, one cypress, no soldiers.
COMPOSITION: The glint on the raised stone is at the exact centre and is the
brightest element, brighter than the serpent's eyes. Both figures stay inside
the central 45% width. The serpent is decorative and no taller than the king
is long. Full-bleed, no border.

### 04-forugh

Characters: grown Hushang, both blocks.

SUBJECT: The stone misses the serpent and strikes another stone. At the exact
centre two rocks crack together and a small white-gold spark bursts, the
first fire. The serpent is off to the side, unhurt, already turning away,
dimmer than the spark. Grown Hushang watches, small, stone just thrown, the
same tunic and one-disc diadem. A little pale dust. No gore. The spark is
young fire, not an explosion and not a sun.
COMPOSITION: The spark is a small white-gold point at the exact centre and is
the only bright thing. Hushang and the two stones stay inside the central 45%
width. The serpent is smaller and to one side. Full-bleed, no border.

### 05-nayesh

Characters: grown Hushang, both blocks. Nobody in the sky.

SUBJECT: He gives thanks, and there is no one to paint in the sky. Grown
Hushang stands, head raised, eyes wet, diadem and leopard tunic unchanged,
knife sheathed. Before him a narrow column of fire, the qibla he has just
chosen. Above the fire the sky is only deep lapis opening into white-gold
light. That light has no face, no hand, no eye, no letter and no body. Two
tiny leopard-clad men stand far back, dim. Night mountain.
COMPOSITION: The white-gold heart of the fire column is at the exact centre
and is the brightest element, brighter than the sky opening. No face anywhere
in the sky. Hushang stays inside the central 45% width, below the fire's
heart. Full-bleed, no border.

### 06-sadeh

Characters: grown Hushang, both blocks.

SUBJECT: The night of Sadeh. A great bonfire, tall as a hill of flame but
still flat gold and vermilion pigment, burns at the centre of a mountain
circle. Grown Hushang stands in the circle with a small group of leopard-clad
men. They hold small cups. No bottles with labels, no written banner, no
name of the feast anywhere. The mood is a quiet feast, not a carnival. Gold
chi clouds low. Lapis night above, darker than the fire.
COMPOSITION: The bonfire's white-gold core is at the exact centre and is the
brightest element. The king and the nearest men stay inside the central 45%
width and are small beside the fire. Full-bleed, no border.

### 07-arreh

Characters: grown Hushang, both blocks.

SUBJECT: The smith's first tools, after the fire. A small gold lamp burns on
a cloth spread over a rock. On the cloth lie two new tools and nothing else:
a short saw and an adze, plain iron, no marks and no letters. Grown Hushang
sits beside them, one hand near but not covering the lamp. Same tunic, same
one-disc diadem, knife sheathed. The furnace of the earlier night is a dim
glow behind him. Night.
COMPOSITION: The lamp's flame is at the exact centre and is the brightest
point, brighter than the iron. Tools and king stay inside the central 45%
width. Full-bleed, no border.

### 08-rud

Characters: grown Hushang, both blocks. Tiny farmers, no faces to compete.

SUBJECT: He leads water out of the seas. Night landscape, flat stacked
miniature. A dark sea at the upper edge, lapis and black. From it a single
bright channel of water runs down through the centre into small fields. The
channel's nearest bend flashes white-gold. Young green shoots, a pale
harvest of unmarked grain, a short joy. Grown Hushang stands small at the
head of the channel. Two tiny men with simple mattocks, no writing on the
tools. Spongy rocks at the sides. No modern canal locks, no bridges of stone
arches with inscriptions.
COMPOSITION: The white-gold flash on the water is at the exact centre and is
the brightest element. Hushang and the channel's bend stay inside the central
45% width. Fields may run toward the edges but stay dimmer. Full-bleed, no
border.

### 09-pust

Characters: grown Hushang, both blocks.

SUBJECT: The herds, and the furs, told without a killing. On one side of a
small lamp: an ox, an ass and a sheep, calm, flat manuscript animals. On the
other, still wild and a little farther: an onager and a deer. Hung on a line,
four pelts only, already taken: a fox, a white ermine, a squirrel, a dark
sable. No blood, no traps springing, no carcass. Grown Hushang stands among
them, small, the same tunic and diadem. Night pasture, one plane tree, gold
chi clouds.
COMPOSITION: The lamp's flame is at the exact centre and is the brightest
point. The nearest animals and the king stay inside the central 45% width.
Pelts are duller than the flame. Full-bleed, no border.

### 10-nam

Characters: grown Hushang, both blocks. He is still, not wounded.

SUBJECT: The end of the reign, with restraint. The stone seat is empty, the
plain one-disc diadem set on the folded leopard skin. Grown Hushang lies
quiet at the foot of the seat, eyes closed, the same face and beard and
tunic, no wound and no blood. A small gold lamp on the ground still burns;
it is low and it is the only brightness. The sky above is empty lapis, no
face, no hand, no writing: the world does not show a face. A bare tree and
one quiet cypress. No funeral procession, no inscription of a name.
COMPOSITION: The lamp's flame is a small white-gold point at the exact centre
and is the brightest element. The empty seat and the still king stay inside
the central 45% width. The sky is darker than the lamp. Full-bleed, no border.
