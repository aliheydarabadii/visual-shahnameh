# 07.02 · فریدون → فرستادن فریدون، جندل را به یمن

Sixty-nine couplets. The forest of Tamishah becomes three cypresses. When Fereydun is about fifty, three sons appear, still unnamed: Salm and Tur by Shahrnaz, Iraj by Arnavaz. They walk before elephants. He calls Jandal and sends him to find three sisters of one mother and one father. Jandal searches the world, finds only Sarv of Yemen, and asks for the three veiled daughters. Sarv wilts like a cut jasmine, bars the door, and hears his warriors: we are not slaves; either refuse, or ask a great gift of the king who brought Zahhak down. Zahhak himself is not painted. The daughters stay veiled. Fereydun's beard, in this section only, has a little grey at the chin. The sons wear no crowns.
