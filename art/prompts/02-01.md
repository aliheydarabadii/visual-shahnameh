# 02.01 · کیومرس → کیومرس: illustration prompts

Seventy-two couplets. The book's first story: Kayumars, first king, lives on the
mountain in leopard skin; the beasts take their custom from him; his son
Siyamak is killed by Ahriman's black div; the court mourns for a year; Sorush
sends them to war; the boy Hushang leads an army of beasts and paris and cuts
off the div's head; Kayumars' days end, and the world is left a fable.

This section sets the faces for every story after it. The character sheet
below is law. Paste the entries for whoever appears into that painting's
prompt, word for word. Do not redesign them between paintings.

The divine is never a figure. When the king prays, the sky is only lapis and
gold light: no face, no hand, no writing. No depiction of God, the Prophet or
Ali. Wrap every prompt in the template from `art/STYLE.md`. Files go in
`art/originals/02-01/`. Reuse `public/art/01-01/layer-abr.webp` as the light
layer; do not paint a new one.

Each centre is the doorway into the next painting. The brightest point is
small, white-gold, and at the exact centre. People stay miniature. Everything
that matters stays inside the central 45% of the width. Full-bleed: no
border, frame, margin, medallion edge or signature. No letters in any script,
no numerals, no inscriptions on tile, cloth, metal or sky. Books, if any,
show only geometric ornament.

## Character sheet

Paste these unchanged whenever that figure appears.

**Kayumars.** An old king at miniature scale. Long narrow face, high forehead,
dark almond eyes, a long straight nose, a small closed mouth. An ivory-white
beard in three points: two side locks and one long centre lock to the chest.
A narrow gold diadem, a simple band with one small plain gold disc at the
front. Never a tall crown, never a turban. A leopard-skin mantle, pale tawny
with black ringed rosettes, over an ivory tunic with a thin turquoise hem.
Bare ankles. A soft pale-gold disc of farr behind the head, always dimmer
than the painting's central light. He always faces the viewer's left.

**Siyamak.** The same face as Kayumars, made young. No beard. The same almond
eyes, the same long nose, a smoother jaw. Dark hair in two long locks past
the shoulders, held by a thin gold fillet with no disc. At court, a
hip-length leopard mantle of the same pale tawny and the same black ringed
rosettes, over an ivory tunic. In battle the chest is bare and the leopard
skin sits only on the shoulders; when he falls, that skin is torn at the
waist. Slender. He always faces the viewer's left.

**Hushang.** A boy of the same house, younger and smaller than Siyamak.
Rounder cheeks, the same almond eyes, the same long nose, no beard. Dark hair
cut to the jaw, held by the same thin gold fillet. Leopard skin worn as a
short belted tunic, the same pale tawny and the same black ringed rosettes,
with ivory trousers. A short knife with an ivory hilt at his belt, sheathed
except when he strikes the div. He always faces the viewer's left.

**The black div.** Wingless. Charcoal-black skin with a dull lapis sheen, not
glossy. A wolf's muzzle, two white tusks, two short horns curving back. A
black mane to the shoulders. The eyes are two small vermilion dots, not
lamps and not white. Three dark claws on each hand. No clothes, no armor.
About twice a man's height, still flat manuscript scale: never a realistic
monster, never a Western demon, never a bat wing. He always faces the
viewer's right, toward the men.

**Sorush.** Not an angel of religious art, and not a divine person. A slender
pari: an oval calm face, dark eyes, no beard, no crown, no halo. A short
leopard cloak, paler tawny than the king's, with the same black ringed
rosettes. Two narrow gold wings drawn as arabesque leaves, not bird feathers.
No weapon. Small.

**Ahriman.** Never a body. Only a tall smudge of lamp-black and ultramarine
behind the black div, with no face, no eyes and no hands.

**The dehqan** (the teller, one painting only; he is not Kayumars). A
middle-aged man with a brown-grey beard, an indigo coat, a plain brown felt
cap. No diadem, no leopard skin, no farr.

### 00-kuh: threshold

No characters.

SUBJECT: The first mountain, before the story is told. Night. Stacked spongy
rocks in lapis, rose, ochre and malachite. A pale path climbs to a cave mouth
at the centre. Inside the cave, a small intense point of white-gold light. A
leopard skin, pale tawny with black ringed rosettes, is folded on a rock
below the cave, empty of any wearer. One cypress. Gold chi clouds along the
lower edge. No people, no animals, no throne yet.
COMPOSITION: The white-gold point inside the cave is at the exact centre of
the frame and is the only bright thing. The cave and the folded skin stay
inside the central 45% width. Rocks run off every edge. Full-bleed, no
border.

### 01-dehqan: سخن گوی دهقان چه گوید نخست / چنین گفت کیین تخت و کلاه

Characters: the dehqan only. Do not show Kayumars.

SUBJECT: An old storyteller, the dehqan, seated on a carpet among spongy
rocks at night, telling the first tale. Brown-grey beard, indigo coat, plain
brown felt cap. No diadem, no leopard skin. Beside him a small gold lamp,
and an open book whose pages carry only a geometric star, never letters.
In the lamp's glow a tiny plain gold diadem seems to appear, as if the book
has just named the first king. A closed book with a geometric cover lies
nearby. Plane leaves. No second person.
COMPOSITION: The lamp's white-gold flame is at the exact centre and is the
brightest point, brighter than the diadem. The dehqan is small, just below
centre. Book and lamp stay inside the central 45% width. Dark lapis sky above.
Full-bleed, no border.

CORRECTION (the first painting failed): remove the European crown with
trefoil points. The diadem is a tiny plain gold band with one small disc, no
points, no cross, no fleurs, and it is dimmer than the flame. Put the white
core of the flame at exact pixel (768, 512). Keep the dehqan, the geometric
books, the carpet and the rocks. No signature.

### 02-hamal: چو آمد به برج حمل آفتاب / پلنگینه پوشید خود با گروه

Characters: Kayumars, exactly as in the sheet. A few tiny followers in the
same leopard skin, too small for faces to compete.

SUBJECT: The world just made young. A gold sun disc at the centre of a deep
lapis sky, with one small decorative ram standing in the sky beside it, dimmer
than the sun, with no glyph and no letters. Below, stacked spongy mountains
in rose, lapis and malachite, a thin spring, a few young shoots. On a rock
seat Kayumars sits in his leopard mantle and narrow gold diadem, small, his
farr dimmer than the sun. Two or three tiny followers in the same leopard
skin sit lower on the rocks. New food is a small bowl of fruit and a loaf
with no marks. It is still night painting: the sun is gold leaf, not daylight.
COMPOSITION: The sun disc's white-gold core is at the exact centre and is the
brightest element, brighter than the ram and brighter than the farr. Kayumars
is inside the central 45% width, below the sun. Mountains run off the edges.
Full-bleed, no border.

### 03-dad: همی تافت زو فر شاهنشهی / به رسم نماز آمدندیش پیش

Characters: Kayumars, exactly as in the sheet. No Siyamak yet.

SUBJECT: The peaceable kingdom on the mountain at night. Kayumars sits on a
low stone throne. His pale-gold farr is behind his head. Around the throne,
small manuscript animals bow or lie quiet: a lion, a leopard, a wolf, an ox,
a ram, two deer, a hoopoe and two doves. They are flat, outlined, decorative,
not realistic wildlife. They face him. Spongy rocks, a cypress, gold chi
clouds, a lapis sky with a few gold rosette stars. No soldiers.
COMPOSITION: A small white-gold point at the heart of the farr, just in front
of his chest, is at the exact centre of the frame and is the brightest
element, brighter than any animal's eye. Kayumars and the nearest beasts stay
inside the central 45% width. Full-bleed, no border.

### 04-siamak: پسر بد مراورا یکی خوبروی / ز بیم جداییش بریان بدی

Characters: Kayumars and Siyamak, exactly as in the sheet. Court dress for
Siyamak. No div.

SUBJECT: Father and son, close, at night on the mountain. Kayumars seated,
Siyamak standing at his knee. The same family face, old and young. A single
small gold lamp between their faces. Kayumars' eyes are wet; one hand is
raised near the boy's shoulder, not covering the face. The leopard skins
match. Behind them the stone throne and one sleeping lion are dim and small.
The mood is affection and the fear of parting. No tears drawn as writing.
COMPOSITION: The lamp's flame is at the exact centre and is the brightest
point. Both faces are inside the central 45% width, on either side of the
lamp, close enough to read as one family. Dark lapis behind them. Full-bleed,
no border.

### 05-div: به رشک اندر آهرمن بدسگال / جهان کرد یکسر پرآوای خویش

Characters: the black div, exactly as in the sheet. Ahriman as a faceless
smudge only. No Kayumars, no Siyamak.

SUBJECT: Jealousy gathering an army. The black div stands huge and wolf-faced,
facing the viewer's right, one claw raised. Behind him a taller smudge of
lamp-black and ultramarine with no face, no eyes and no hands: Ahriman, not a
body. A host of smaller divs, the same charcoal skin and short horns, much
smaller, carry no banners with marks. They march across a dark mountain
toward a distant tiny gold glow, the throne they want. The ground is
ultramarine dust. Gold is scarce. Vermilion only in the div's two small eyes
and nowhere else.
COMPOSITION: The distant gold glow of the throne is at the exact centre of
the frame and is the brightest element, small and white-gold, brighter than
the vermilion eyes. The black div's body stays inside the central half, left
of that glow, facing it. Full-bleed, no border.

CORRECTION (the first painting failed): the div was a giant nude bodybuilder
cut off by the left edge, with long horns and too many claws, and the gold
clouds were nearly as bright as the centre. Match the div in
`art/originals/02-01/07-razm.png`: shaggy, wolf-headed, short horns, flat
manuscript scale, about twice a man, fully inside the frame and inside the
central half. The only white-gold light is a small point at exact pixel
(768, 512), not a domed shrine. Gold clouds stay dull ochre. No religious
building.

### 06-sorush: یکایک بیامد خجسته سروش / بپوشید تن را به چرم پلنگ

Characters: Sorush, Kayumars and Siyamak, each exactly as in the sheet.
Siyamak is arming: leopard skin on the shoulders, ivory tunic half on.

SUBJECT: The warning, inside the mountain cave at night. Sorush, the slender
pari in a pale leopard cloak with two narrow arabesque gold wings, stands at
the centre and speaks. Kayumars sits listening, diadem and long white beard.
Below him Siyamak pulls the leopard skin onto his shoulders; there is no
armor anywhere in the picture, no helmet, no mail. A few tiny men in leopard
skin gather in the dark behind Siyamak. No div in this painting.
COMPOSITION: A white-gold point between Sorush's wings is at the exact centre
and is the brightest element, brighter than the wings. All three faces stay
inside the central 45% width. The cave dark runs to the edges. Full-bleed, no
border.

### 07-razm: سیامک بیامد برهنه تنا / تبه گشت و ماند انجمن بی‌خدیو

Characters: Siyamak and the black div, exactly as in the sheet. Siyamak is
bare-chested, leopard skin on the shoulders only, fillet still in his hair.

SUBJECT: The first death, told as a manuscript battle, not a horror image.
Siyamak, slender and bare-chested, grapples the black div. The div's claw has
caught him at the waist and the leopard skin there is torn. Siyamak is
folding, eyes closing, face still recognisable, fillet still on. A small
vermilion line at the waist, no gore, no viscera. Tiny hosts of leopard-clad
men on one side and small dark divs on the other, far back, dim. Night
mountains. The men face left, the div faces right.
COMPOSITION: A small white-gold burst where the claw meets the waist is at
the exact centre and is the brightest point. Both faces stay inside the
central 45% width. Vermilion is only that one small line. Full-bleed, no
border.

### 08-sug: چو آگه شد از مرگ فرزند شاه / دو دیده پر از نم چو ابر بهار

Characters: Kayumars, exactly as in the sheet. No Siyamak's body. No div.

SUBJECT: A father who has just heard. Kayumars has stepped down from the
stone throne. The throne behind him is empty. He stands bent, both hands at
his white beard, cheeks marked by two small vermilion streaks, eyes wet. The
leopard mantle and the narrow diadem are unchanged. One gold lamp has fallen
onto its side at his feet and still burns. A few courtiers in leopard skin
are tiny, dim, hands at their faces, not at the centre. Night. The mountain
court has gone quiet.
COMPOSITION: The fallen lamp's flame is at the exact centre and is the
brightest point. Kayumars' face is just above it, inside the central 45%
width. The empty throne is dimmer than the lamp. Full-bleed, no border.

### 09-piruzeh: همه جامه‌ها کرده پیروزه رنگ / نشستند سالی چنین سوگوار

Characters: none of the four principals large. If Kayumars appears he is a
tiny figure at the gate, still in his diadem and leopard skin.

SUBJECT: Public mourning leaving the mountain court. A procession of small
figures in turquoise robes, faces pale like citron, eyes down. Among them
and ahead of them, animals walk wailing toward a high mountain path: lion,
leopard, wolf, deer, an ox, hoopoes. Dust, painted as pale gold powder, rises
from the gate. At the end of the path a single distant point of white-gold
light, the message that has not yet arrived. No Sorush yet. Turquoise cloth
is the mourning colour. One year is a bare tree and a tree in faint leaf,
side by side, small, not labelled.
COMPOSITION: The distant white-gold point at the head of the path is at the
exact centre and is the brightest element. The procession occupies the lower
centre. Key figures inside the central 45% width. Full-bleed, no border.

### 10-payam: درود آوریدش خجسته سروش / بر آن برترین نام یزدانش را

Characters: Kayumars and Sorush, exactly as in the sheet.

SUBJECT: The command, and a prayer with nobody in the sky. Kayumars stands
on the mountain, head raised, lashes wet, diadem and leopard mantle unchanged.
Sorush stands a little aside, gold arabesque wings, pale leopard cloak, one
hand extended in speech, no weapon. Above them a deep lapis sky opens in a
white-gold light. The light has no face, no hand, no eye, no letter and no
name written in it. The king looks up into that light. A few tiny leopard-clad
men begin to stand, dim, at the edges.
COMPOSITION: The white-gold opening in the sky is at the exact centre of the
frame and is the brightest element, brighter than Sorush's wings. No face
anywhere in the sky. Kayumars and Sorush stay inside the central 45% width.
Full-bleed, no border.

### 11-hushang: خجسته سیامک یکی پور داشت / جز او بر کسی چشم نگماشتی

Characters: Kayumars and Hushang, exactly as in the sheet. The knife stays
sheathed. No Siyamak. No div.

SUBJECT: The boy who is all that remains. Kayumars seated on the stone
throne, one arm around Hushang. Hushang is smaller, rounder-cheeked, hair to
the jaw, the same almond eyes and long nose, leopard skin as a short tunic,
ivory trousers, sheathed ivory-hilt knife. A single gold lamp between them.
The old king's eyes are on the boy and on no one else. One empty place at the
throne's other side, where a son would have stood: a folded leopard skin and
nothing else. Night mountain, quiet.
COMPOSITION: The lamp's flame is at the exact centre and is the brightest
point. Both faces are inside the central 45% width. The empty place is dim.
Full-bleed, no border.

### 12-lashkar: پری و پلنگ انجمن کرد و شیر / نبیره به پیش اندرون با سپاه

Characters: Hushang and Kayumars, exactly as in the sheet. Hushang's knife
stays sheathed. Small paris use Sorush's wing shape but are much smaller and
have no individual importance.

SUBJECT: The army setting out across the night mountains. In front, Hushang
on foot, small, leopard tunic, facing left. Behind him, higher in the stacked
space and smaller, Kayumars on foot in his mantle and diadem. Around them a
manuscript host: lions, leopards, wolves, one tiger, deer, oxen, hoopoes, and
a few tiny paris with narrow arabesque gold wings and pale leopard cloaks.
No banners with marks. A single sun-disc standard on a plain pole, gold, with
no inscription, shines at the centre of the march. Dust rises. No div yet.
COMPOSITION: The sun-disc standard's white-gold core is at the exact centre
and is the brightest element. Hushang is just below it, Kayumars just above,
both inside the central 45% width. Animals may spill toward the edges but
must not outshine the disc. Full-bleed, no border.

### 13-kineh: بیامد سیه دیو با ترس و باک / سپهبد برید آن سر بی‌همال

Characters: Hushang and the black div, exactly as in the sheet. Kayumars may
appear only as a tiny watcher behind the host. The knife is drawn, ivory
hilt, short pale blade.

SUBJECT: The revenge, as a Safavid battle page, not a gore picture. The black
div has arrived afraid: same wolf muzzle, same horns, same vermilion-dot
eyes, shoulders drawn in. The two hosts have met: beasts and tiny paris
against small dark divs. Hushang reaches like a lion and the short knife
meets the div's neck. The div's head is just coming away as a dark shape,
lowered, with one small vermilion line and no spray. The body is still
upright and monstrous, not anatomical. Hushang's face is calm and
recognisable, fillet on, leopard tunic unchanged.
COMPOSITION: A white-gold flash at the knife's edge is at the exact centre
and is the brightest point. Both faces stay inside the central 45% width.
The div faces right, Hushang faces left. Full-bleed, no border.

### 14-fasan: چو آمد مر آن کینه را خواستار / جهان سربه‌سر چو فسانست و بس

Characters: Kayumars, exactly as in the sheet, or his mantle if he is already
still. No div. No body of the div.

SUBJECT: The king's days end. The stone throne on the mountain is empty. The
leopard mantle is folded on it, and the narrow gold diadem with its one disc
rests on the skin. Kayumars himself is a small still figure lying as if
asleep at the foot of the throne, eyes closed, beard and face unchanged, hands
quiet, no wound. A last white-gold point of light hovers just above the empty
seat and is fading. Around the throne the animals sit quiet, heads low: lion,
leopard, a deer. The world has gone still. A few gold rosette stars. No
procession, no battle.
COMPOSITION: The fading white-gold point above the empty seat is at the exact
centre and is the brightest element. Throne, mantle and the king's face stay
inside the central 45% width. Dark lapis at the corners, full-bleed, no
border. The light is a doorway, small enough that the next thing could open
out of it.
